Section 05 — Website Conversion & User Experience

Getting Traffic Is One Thing. Converting It Is Another.

Beyond speed, your website has several conversion barriers that are causing visitors — especially new patients — to leave without booking an appointment. These are fixable, and fixing them will directly increase the number of calls and form submissions you receive from the same traffic you're already getting.

Issue

Too Many Stock Photos

Generic stock imagery signals inauthenticity to patients comparing multiple dentists. Real photos of your team, your office, and your actual patients (with consent) build trust and dramatically increase conversion rates. Studies show practices with authentic imagery convert 2–3× better than those with stock photos.

Issue

Header Is Too Narrow & Navigation Too Small

The top section of the website is visually tight and the navigation text is small. For your demographic — which includes many older adults seeking family and restorative dentistry — small text and a compact header creates friction. A clear, spacious header with a prominent "Book Appointment" button is essential.

Issue

No FAQ Sections on Treatment Pages

Treatment pages (implants, Invisalign, whitening, etc.) lack FAQ sections. This hurts in two ways: (1) Google uses FAQs to understand page depth and relevance, and (2) AI platforms like ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews pull directly from FAQ-style content to answer patient questions. No FAQs = invisible to AI search.

Issue

No /llms.txt File

An /llms.txt file tells AI systems (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) how to understand and represent your practice. Without it, AI platforms may describe your practice inaccurately or skip you entirely. This is a simple addition — one file — that future-proofs your AI visibility.

Section 06 — AI Search Visibility

The New Battlefield: AI Platforms

ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity are now answering dental questions for millions of patients every day. When someone asks "who is the best dentist in Santa Maria for dental implants?" — the answer they get comes from AI, not a list of blue links. If your website isn't structured for AI consumption, you won't appear in those answers.

AI Readiness Assessment

Three Things Blocking Your AI Visibility

  • /llms.txt missing: AI crawlers don't have a clear guide to your practice. Add this file to your domain root to tell AI systems who you are, what you do, and what content to surface.
  • No FAQ content on service pages: AI models extract Q&A pairs to answer user questions. Your implant, whitening, and Invisalign pages need structured FAQ sections with real patient questions.
  • No blog = no AI training data: Regular blog posts about dental topics give AI platforms content to associate with your practice. Practices publishing 4+ posts/month consistently show up in AI-generated recommendations.
