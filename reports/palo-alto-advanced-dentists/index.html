Executive Summary

Key Findings

⚠️

Single-Page Site — Cannot Rank for Any Keyword

The new website has everything on one page with no dedicated service pages. Google cannot rank a single page for multiple keywords. It is architecturally impossible for this site to hold or grow the 711+ keyword rankings the old site had built.

🤖

Looks AI-Generated — Damages Trust & Conversions

The new design doesn't look like a real dental practice website. It lacks authentic photos, patient testimonials, doctor bios, and the visual trust signals patients expect. Visitors who land on it are unlikely to convert to booked appointments.

📉

711 Keywords at Risk of Being Lost

The old ProSites website was indexed across dozens of pages — each targeting different services and keywords. When Google re-crawls and finds those URLs either gone or redirected to a thin homepage, those rankings will collapse. This is not hypothetical — it's a predictable outcome.

🔍

529 Visits/Month — Almost Entirely Branded

Of the ~529 organic monthly sessions, the majority come from people searching the practice name directly. Commercial-intent searches — “dentist palo alto,” “dental implants palo alto,” “periodontist palo alto” — bring in minimal traffic because the content to capture them no longer exists.

📊

Authority Score 10/100 — Bottom of Market

With 192 referring domains but an Authority Score of just 10, the practice's backlink profile is thin relative to Palo Alto competitors. This makes it even harder to recover rankings quickly once the content gaps are filled.

🏥

Strong Service Footprint — None of It Online

The practice offers implants, periodontics, orthodontics, cosmetic dentistry, emergency care, and more. These are high-value, high-search-volume services — but none have a dedicated page. All that revenue opportunity is invisible online.

Question 4 of 4
04
What Can Dentist Nerds Do to Help?

A structured rescue and growth plan in four phases. Phase 1 stops the bleeding immediately. Phase 2 builds the new foundation. Phase 3 expands visibility. Phase 4 compounds with ongoing SEO and reporting.

Phase 1 · Days 1–14 — Emergency Rescue

01

Archive the Old Website

Pull the full ProSites site from the Wayback Machine (web.archive.org). Recover every service page, blog post, and URL structure before they fade from Google's index. This is time-sensitive — every week of delay costs rankings.

02

Map Old URLs → New Site Plan

Create a complete redirect map and new site architecture that mirrors the old URL structure where possible. This preserves link equity and tells Google exactly where the content has moved. Prevents any remaining ranking loss from the redesign.

03

New Website Design Brief

The new site needs to look and feel like a premium Palo Alto dental practice — not AI-generated. We'll define a proper design standard: professional photography, authentic doctor bios, patient trust signals, service-page structure, and conversion-optimized CTAs.

Phase 2 · Days 15–45 — Rebuild the Foundation

04

Rebuild Service Pages & Blogs

Recreate all service pages from the old site — implants, immediate implants, periodontics, gum grafting, cosmetic dentistry, orthodontics/braces, Invisalign, emergency dentistry, endodontics, bone grafting, sinus lift, and more. Recover and update all blog posts. Each page gets proper title tags, meta descriptions, H1/H2 structure, and FAQ schema.

05

Deploy Schema & E-E-A-T Markup

Add Dentist + LocalBusiness schema, Doctor/Person schema for the practice physicians, FAQ schema on every service page, and breadcrumb schema. This is critical for both Google rankings and AI Overview citations — and the site currently has none of it.

06

Google Business Profile Optimization

Fully optimize the GBP with complete services, photos, appointment links, Q&A, and a review acquisition system. Manage the listing monthly. A well-maintained GBP is the fastest way to appear in the Local Pack for “dentist palo alto” (390/mo), which drives high-intent new patients.

Phase 3 · Days 46–90 — Expand Visibility

07

Backlink Audit & Cleanup

Audit all 764 backlinks for toxicity and low quality. Disavow harmful links dragging down the Authority Score. Then build new high-authority citations — Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Vitals, 1-800-Dentist, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Nextdoor, Palo Alto Chamber of Commerce, and local press.

08

Link Building Campaign

Target high-DA dental directories, Stanford area business directories, and local Palo Alto/Silicon Valley press for practice feature mentions. The goal is lifting Authority Score from 10 to 18–22 within 12 months, which meaningfully improves ranking positions across all keywords site-wide.

09

Optimize for AI Visibility

Structure content to answer the exact questions patients ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews: “best dentist in palo alto,” “how much do dental implants cost palo alto,” “what is pinhole gum surgery.” FAQ schema + clear, authoritative answers = free AI visibility that competitors aren't pursuing yet.

Phase 4 · Ongoing — Monthly SEO & Reporting

10

Monthly Performance Reports

Every month: organic traffic, keyword position tracking, GBP performance, review count, new backlinks acquired, and AI visibility indicators. You'll always know exactly what's working and what's next.

11

Ongoing Content Engine

1–2 new blog posts per month targeting Palo Alto patient questions, seasonal dental topics, and long-tail keyword opportunities. Every post links to a service page and is formatted for AI Overview citation. This compounds month over month.

12

GBP Monthly Management

Weekly GBP posts, photo uploads, Q&A management, review responses, and appointment link maintenance. Active GBP management is the single highest-ROI local SEO activity for a dental practice — it directly drives calls and booked appointments.

Projected 12-month outcome: By protecting and rebuilding the old content, the practice holds its existing 705-keyword footprint and expands it. Commercial search share grows from 6.4% → 18–24%. Organic traffic grows from 529/mo → 1,200–1,800/mo with growth coming from service pages, not just the homepage. GBP Local Pack presence captures 30–50% of “dentist palo alto” and “dental implants palo alto” searches. Authority Score lifts to 18–22. All of this is reported monthly so you can see the ROI clearly.
