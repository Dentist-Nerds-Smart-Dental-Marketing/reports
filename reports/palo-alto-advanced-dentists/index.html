Critical Warning

The New Website Is Putting Years of SEO at Risk

The practice recently switched from a ProSites site with many pages to a new single-page website. The old site ranked for 711 keywords with roughly 500 monthly visitors. The new site has ONE page and appears AI-generated — no service pages, no doctor bios, no trust signals. Google will begin devaluing those old URL rankings within weeks. Without action to recreate those pages, years of SEO equity will be lost.

Time-sensitive: The window to recover the old site from archive and rebuild before rankings collapse is 60–90 days. Every week of delay erodes recoverable equity.
Key Findings

Six Findings That Demand Action

The new site looks clean on the surface, but structurally it cannot hold the rankings the old site earned. Here is what the data shows.

1
Single-Page Site — Cannot Rank
Everything is on one page. Google cannot rank a single URL for multiple keywords. It is architecturally impossible to hold the 711+ keyword rankings on one page.
2
Looks AI-Generated — Damages Trust
No authentic photos, no doctor bios, no patient testimonials. Visitors won't convert to booked appointments when the site signals are this thin.
3
711 Keywords at Immediate Risk
Old ProSites URLs still indexed act as a temporary ranking buffer. Once Google re-crawls and finds them gone, all rankings collapse. There is a 60–90 day window.
4
529 Visits / Month — Almost All Branded
Nearly all traffic is from patients who already know the practice name. Commercial-intent searches (implants, periodontics, dentist palo alto) bring minimal traffic.
5
Authority Score 10/100
Thin relative to local competitors. 192 referring domains but low quality, which makes recovery slower once content is rebuilt.
6
Strong Services — Zero Online Visibility
Implants, periodontics, orthodontics, cosmetic, emergency care — all offered, none rankable. Every high-value service is invisible online.
