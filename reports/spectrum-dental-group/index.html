Executive Summary

Key Findings

Strong Foundation: 1,308 Keywords & a Real Content Engine
This is the rare practice that has built genuine organic momentum — from ~150 keywords two years ago to 1,308 today, peaking at 1,198 monthly visits in late 2025. Blog content like "what does a toothache feel like" and "molars in toddlers" ranks top 3. The foundation is real; the job now is to stop the leaks and capture the high-value commercial terms.
Usability Flaws Are Quietly Costing Bookings
The hero has no call-to-action button and its text contrast is too low to read comfortably. Six service-card images are broken. The footer hours layout is inconsistent and the footer background image hurts contrast. For a practice already earning 650+ visits a month, every one of these is friction between an interested patient and a booked appointment.
The Biggest Money Keywords Sit on Legacy "-old" Pages
"Dental implants prices" (18,100/mo, pos 23), "tooth implant cost" (9,900/mo, pos 21) and "cost of dental implants" (9,900/mo, pos 33) all rank from /dental-implants-old/cost/ — a deprecated URL left over from a past migration. The highest-intent, highest-volume terms are stranded on page 2–3 on pages that should have been rebuilt and consolidated.
No FAQ Content or Schema on Treatment Pages — Hurting AI Visibility
The treatment pages have no FAQ sections and no FAQ/Service schema. This is a primary reason the practice is underrepresented in AI search — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews rely on structured Q&A content to understand, trust and cite a dental practice.
Missing & Fragmented Treatment / Service Pages
The site offers implants, cosmetic, pediatric, sedation, orthodontics and more, but the architecture is fragmented — current and legacy "-old" versions of the same service compete, and several treatments lack a clean, optimized, schema-rich landing page. A consolidated, complete treatment-page map is needed to capture demand the practice isn't yet ranking for.
Backlink Profile Needs Evaluation
200 referring domains and Authority Score 20 — a solid base — but only 390 of 1,076 backlinks are "follow," and the profile has never been deliberately built. A full evaluation plus targeted high-authority link-building is needed to push the money pages from page 2 onto page 1.
