ISSUE 1 Critical · Zero Traffic

Zero organic traffic — the website is essentially invisible to anyone who doesn't already know the practice name.

Semrush reports 0 monthly organic visits to flowdentistryhb.com. The site ranks for 18 keywords, but every single one is either a branded term (doctor names, practice name variations) or a generic term ranking in positions 40–90 — positions where no one clicks. No organic traffic means the website exists purely as a digital business card, not as a patient acquisition channel.

For a practice in Huntington Beach — one of the most desirable coastal markets in Southern California — this represents an enormous missed opportunity. Patients searching "dentist Huntington Beach," "teeth whitening HB," or "dental implants near me" are not finding Flow Dentistry at all.

✓ The Fix

Build a new website with full treatment pages optimized for Huntington Beach and surrounding area searches (Fountain Valley, Costa Mesa, Westminster, Seal Beach). Each treatment page targets its own set of keywords — implants, veneers, Invisalign, whitening, crowns, emergency dental — so the site grows a keyword footprint across every service the practice offers, not just branded searches.

ISSUE 2 Critical · No Service Pages

No treatment pages means Google has nothing to rank — and patients have nothing to read before they decide to book.

The current site has a single /services/ page — no individual pages for implants, veneers, Invisalign, crowns, whitening, emergency dental, cleanings, or any other treatment. Google can only rank pages that exist. Without dedicated treatment pages, the site can never appear when a patient searches for a specific procedure, no matter how good the general site content is.

This is the most direct explanation for zero organic traffic. There is simply no content for Google to show.

✓ The Fix

The new website will include a full library of treatment pages — at minimum: dental implants, porcelain veneers, teeth whitening, Invisalign / clear aligners, dental crowns & bridges, emergency dentistry, preventive care, and cosmetic dentistry overview. Each page: 700–1,200 words of original content, local keywords, FAQ section with schema markup for AI Overview visibility, and a clear call to action. Every page becomes its own ranking opportunity.

ISSUE 3 Critical · Toxic Backlinks

652 backlinks but Domain Authority of 2 — a classic sign of a spam-poisoned link profile that's actively hurting rankings.

A healthy website with 652 backlinks would typically have a Domain Authority of 20–40+. flowdentistryhb.com has a DA of 2. That mismatch tells a clear story: the overwhelming majority of those 652 links are from spammy, low-quality, or penalized websites — the kind of links that Google treats as a negative trust signal rather than a positive one.

Toxic backlinks don't just fail to help — they actively suppress rankings. Google's Penguin algorithm specifically devalues or penalizes sites with manipulative link profiles. Until these links are cleaned up, every other SEO effort is working against a headwind.

✓ The Fix

Run a full toxic backlink audit using Semrush's Backlink Audit tool. Identify all harmful links (spam score 60+), attempt removal outreach to those domains, and submit a Google Disavow file for all links that can't be removed. Simultaneously begin building clean, high-authority links from dental associations, local Huntington Beach business directories, and Orange County health organizations. Target DA 20+ within 12 months.

ISSUE 4 High · Conversion Gap

The website isn't built to convert — no treatment info, no pricing context, no FAQ content, no clear path to book.

A prospective patient who finds Flow Dentistry on Google Maps, reads 226 five-star reviews, and clicks through to the website encounters a site with no treatment detail, no FAQ answers, no before/after photos, and no patient-facing content that helps them make a booking decision. The conversion path drops off immediately.

For a practice at this review level, patients are interested and motivated — the website just needs to be there to close it. A patient researching dental implants in Huntington Beach expects to find a page explaining the procedure, the process, the cost range, recovery, and FAQs — not a generic landing page.

✓ The Fix

The new website is built for conversion first: each treatment page has a prominent booking CTA, patient-friendly content answering the questions patients actually ask, FAQ schema for AI search visibility, and a layout optimized for mobile (where most HB dental searches happen). We embed the Google review count prominently on the homepage to immediately leverage those 226 five-star reviews the moment a visitor lands.
