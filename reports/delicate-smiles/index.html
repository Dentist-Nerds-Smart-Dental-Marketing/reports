Brand, design & content quality

Most traffic is branded — what they land on is hurting conversion

The bulk of the 313 monthly visits comes from brand searches — people who already know "Delicate Smiles" and are looking the practice up. Those visitors arrive pre-qualified and high-intent. But what they land on — a dated visual brand, heavy stock imagery, and thin service pages with little structure — is leaking conversion. The work here has outsized leverage because the audience is already at the door.

Recommendation 01 — Brand identity

Refresh the visual brand — logo, tagline, color scheme

The current logo, tagline, and color scheme don't match the quality of the practice or the reputation built on 4.9★ and 85 Google reviews. A refined visual identity is the foundation a cosmetic site redesign builds on — and it compounds every other improvement in this audit.

Recommendation 02 — Imagery

Replace stock photos with real practice photography

Most pages currently rely on generic stock dental imagery. Real photography — of the doctors, team, office, and patient cases (with consent) — builds trust faster than copy can. Critical for cosmetic, implant, and Invisalign pages where patients want to see actual work, not models.

Recommendation 03 — Page structure

Match every service page to the emergency-dentist page — then exceed it

Every service page needs revamping — proper HTML heading hierarchy, FAQs, 1,000+ words of practice-specific content, and schema for AI visibility. The proof is already on the site: /dental-emergencies-and-pain-relief ranks #9 for "emergency dentist milwaukee" (1,000/mo) because it has more content and more structural work than any other service page. That page is the bar. Bring all six others up to match — then exceed it with MedicalProcedure schema, galleries, and cost guidance.

The recommendation

Build a new website

The cosmetic improvements needed — brand refresh, real photography, content rebuilt on every service page, proper HTML and schema structure — add up to a full ground-up rebuild, not incremental patches. The technical foundation is working (22 pages indexed, architecture solid), but the front-end experience that converts brand traffic into bookings needs to start clean. A new site is the highest-leverage move available to the practice.

Optional recommendation · Domain

The current domain is delicatesmiles.net. A .com typically builds more trust with patients and carries a slight SEO edge. The current .net already has real authority built up (175 referring domains, Authority Score 17), so this isn't a priority — but if delicatesmiles.com is available, it's worth considering as part of the rebuild.

Technical Issues

Site-level technical SEO health check. Counts derived from a crawl of the indexed page set and on-page review of homepage + four representative service pages.

7
Service Pages Without Schema

None of the seven service pages carry MedicalProcedure, LocalBusiness, or FAQPage schema. Schema is the channel through which Google AI Overviews and entity search pick up procedure-level data.

7
Thin Service-Page Copy

Service pages run 500–900 words of largely templated content. No before/after galleries, no transparent cost ranges, no procedure-specific FAQs, no embedded patient testimonials.

4
Indexed URLs to Clean Up

One 404 (/botox), two legacy 301s with no live equivalent destination, and one Hibu template splash page diluting crawl signals.

22
Pages Indexed (Healthy)

Key pages are crawlable and indexed by Google. The architecture is solid — content optimization and internal-link density are the gaps, not technical access.

Highest-leverage technical work: on-page schema (LocalBusiness on the homepage, MedicalProcedure on each service page, AggregateRating sourced from the 85 Google reviews, FAQPage on /faqs) and 4 URL-cleanup tasks. These two combine to unlock service-page eligibility for the AI Overviews and rich-result panels that brand and emergency-dentist queries currently trigger.
