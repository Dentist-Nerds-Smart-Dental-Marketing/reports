ISSUE 1 Critical · GBP NAP Conflict

The phone number is identical to the parent Pearl Dental Group practice.

Both the Baton Rouge Dental Implant Center website and the parent Pearl Dental Group main listing use the exact same phone number: (225) 277-3311. Google uses Name + Address + Phone (NAP) to differentiate businesses. With identical phones at slightly varying addresses (7017 Perkins Rd vs. 7017 Perkins Rd Suite B), Google can struggle to tell the two GBPs apart — which risks one suppressing the other or both ranking weaker than they should. Same root issue we identified in the Pearl Dental Group main audit.

✓ The fix — Dentist Nerds installs a dedicated call-tracking number

Provision a new dedicated phone number for the Baton Rouge Dental Implant Center site and GBP. The new number rings through to the same front desk as (225) 277-3311 — no calls are lost, the patient experience stays the same. But Google now sees two distinct business entities with their own NAP signatures, so each one can rank cleanly without competing against the other. The new number also gives you per-site analytics: you can finally see how many calls the sister site is driving vs. the parent. Dentist Nerds handles end-to-end setup — number provisioning, GBP updates, schema updates, website updates, analytics dashboard.

ISSUE 2 Critical · Trust Signals

Patient testimonials use AI-generated images — the filenames literally say "ChatGPT-Image".

The "Real Patient Experiences" section displays four glowing testimonials from "Emily K." (Louisiana), "Isabella" (University of Melbourne Medical Student), "Michael R." (Baton Rouge), and "James T." (Baton Rouge). Two of those four headshots are stored on the site under filenames like:

These are AI-generated portraits, downloaded from ChatGPT, and the original filename has been preserved. Beyond the question of whether the testimonials themselves are real, this is a discoverable trust failure — anyone (a patient, a competitor, a journalist, Google's quality team) who right-clicks a testimonial photo sees "ChatGPT-Image" in the filename. The "Isabella, University of Melbourne Medical Student" claim is also suspicious — an Australian medical student isn't a likely Baton Rouge dental implant patient.

✓ The fix

Remove all four AI-generated testimonials immediately. Replace with real patient stories pulled from Pearl Dental Group's existing library — the parent practice has hundreds of real Google reviews and several real video patient stories that can be embedded here too. If real Baton Rouge Dental Implant Center patients want to provide on-site testimonials and headshots later, those can replace the placeholder content. But the AI-generated content has to come down — it's a trust signal that breaks the moment anyone looks at the source.

ISSUE 3 High · Site Depth

Only six implant treatment pages — and most of the rest of the site drifts into cosmetic dentistry.

Counting the actual implant-specific treatment pages, the site has six: Home, All-on-X Implants, Dental Implants For Seniors, Full-Mouth Dental Implants, Cost of Dental Implants, and Before & After. Most of the remaining content on the site drifts into cosmetic dentistry topics — veneers, smile makeovers, general dental care references. For a domain branded as a "Dental Implant Center," two problems compound: (1) the implant coverage is too thin to demonstrate topical authority to Google for the implant niche, and (2) the cosmetic dentistry content dilutes the implant-specialist signal — Google can't decide whether this site is an implant center or a general cosmetic dental site. Result: Authority Score 8 and only 86 ranking keywords.

✓ The fix

Two moves, done together. First, expand the implant content from 6 pages → 15–20 implant-focused pages over the next 3–4 months. Suggested additions: Single-Tooth Implants · Implant-Supported Bridges · Implant-Supported Dentures · Mini Dental Implants · Zygomatic Implants · Bone Grafting & Sinus Lifts · Same-Day Implants · Implant FAQs · Implant Recovery Guide · Implant Financing & Insurance · Veterans & Senior Implant Programs · Implants vs. Bridges · Implants vs. Dentures · Implant Aftercare.

Second, relocate the cosmetic dentistry content. The CD-focused pages belong on Pearl Dental Group's main site — that's the cosmetic + general dentistry brand. 301-redirect each CD page from this sister site to its equivalent on pearldentalgroup.com. The sister site stays cleanly implant-focused; the parent site gets the cosmetic content where it actually belongs. Google can finally read this domain as a specialty implant center, not a generic dental practice.

ISSUE 4 High · Conversion / Media

Stock imagery throughout — hurts conversion on high-value implant decisions.

Most of the photography on the site is generic dental stock imagery: a generic "dental implant consultation" photo, generic "professional dentist portrait" shots, generic cross-section diagrams. For a treatment where patients are evaluating a $5K–$50K decision and choosing between local providers, stock photos undercut the entire trust signal. Patients want to see the actual doctors, the actual office, the actual technology, and actual treatment results.

✓ The fix

Pull real media from Pearl Dental Group's existing library. The parent site already has: real Dr. André Bruni and Dr. Jessica Bruni portraits, real office interior shots, real amenity photos (coffee bar, arcade, prize machines), the in-house lab, real 3D imaging equipment, and several real patient video stories. None of these need to be re-shot — they need to be brought over to the sister site. This is the lowest-cost, highest-impact conversion fix available. Stock-image-only sites convert worse than real-photography sites on every high-consideration medical purchase. Real media is already produced; just use it here too.

ISSUE 5 High · Authority & Backlinks

Very low domain authority — only 22 referring domains.

Semrush shows Authority Score at 8/100 with 28 total backlinks from 22 referring domains. For comparison, the Pearl Dental Group parent has 606 referring domains. This sister site is essentially starting from zero on link equity, which is why even with a strong exact-match domain and clear implant focus, it's only ranking for 86 keywords and pulling 134 monthly visits.

✓ The fix

This is the headline gap, and it directly affects the next two layers: keyword reach (only 86 keywords ranking — needs more pages plus the authority to rank them) and AI search visibility (currently zero — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews don't cite this site for any Baton Rouge implant queries). All three problems share a single root cause: not enough authoritative signals pointing at the domain.

The full plan is laid out in the Authority & AI Visibility Strategy section directly below. The headline moves: internal linking from Pearl Dental Group main (which has 606 referring domains), implant industry directories (AAID, ICOI, NobelBiocare, Straumann), local Baton Rouge citations, editorial pitches to dental industry publications, HARO journalist outreach, and guest content on respected dental blogs.

ISSUE 6 Medium · Platform & Schema

Built on Elementor — slower, bloated code, missing schema markup.

The page-generator tag shows Elementor as the underlying builder. Same as the Pearl Dental Group parent — Elementor sites tend to load more slowly than purpose-built ones because of the extra wrapper divs, inline styles, and helper scripts the builder outputs. There's also no comprehensive schema markup: no Dentist + LocalBusiness, no Physician schema for Dr. Bruni or Dr. Hrebeshchenko, no MedicalProcedure schema on the implant pages, no FAQPage even where FAQs already exist on the homepage. AI search engines lean heavily on schema to decide which sources to cite — without it, the content is harder to surface.

✓ The fix

This is lower priority than the trust and content fixes — the technical foundation isn't blocking growth at this scale. But two quick wins: (1) add the full schema suite (Dentist + LocalBusiness, Physician per doctor, MedicalProcedure on each treatment page, FAQPage on the homepage), which is a 1–2 day implementation and meaningful uplift for AI-search visibility, and (2) if the parent Pearl Dental Group rebuild happens (per the recommendation in that audit), migrate this sister site onto the same modern stack at the same time. One project, two cleaner sites.

Layer 3 · AI Search Visibility

Move from invisible to citable in AI search engines.

AI search isn't a separate channel from traditional SEO — it sits on top of it. The same authority signals (backlinks, mentions, schema, content depth) that move organic rankings also feed the citation engines that ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok, and Google AI Overviews use to decide which sources to surface. A site with zero authority is invisible to AI search by default. The work in Layers 1 and 2 is what unlocks this layer.

Q&A-Structured Content

AI engines lift answers from pages that are already structured as questions and answers. Every treatment page gets a thorough FAQ section. Every blog post is built around a specific question patients ask, with a clear, concise answer in the first paragraph.

Full Schema Markup

FAQPage, MedicalProcedure, Physician, Dentist + LocalBusiness, AggregateRating tied to the GBP. Schema is how AI engines understand what kind of entity a page describes — without it, content is invisible to citation pipelines.

Cross-Publication Mentions

AI engines weight entities that appear consistently across multiple authoritative sources — industry publications, news articles, dental directories, partner pages. The backlink work in Layer 1 doubles as AI-citation prep.

Direct Answer Optimization

Concise, factual, sourced answers in the first 50–100 words of every page. Bullet-point structures for comparisons. Tables for cost or timeline data. AI engines extract whatever they can quickly parse — make sure that's the right information.

Why this matters: AI search is no longer optional. Patients now ask ChatGPT or Perplexity "Who's the best dental implant dentist in Baton Rouge?" — and the AI cites whatever entities have the strongest authority + content + schema combination. Right now Baton Rouge Dental Implant Center is invisible in those answers. As Layers 1 and 2 compound, the site becomes a candidate for inclusion in the citation pool that AI search engines draw from for premium Baton Rouge implant questions.
