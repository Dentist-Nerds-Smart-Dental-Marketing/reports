Our Game Plan

How Dentist Nerds Fixes This

We don't believe in flashy launches that flame out. The plan starts with a strong foundation, then layers ongoing engines on top — each one compounding the last.

⚡ Start Here
01
The Foundation
Deepen the treatment pages & own the ICON cluster.

What we'd do in Step 1

This site is not a rebuild — it's a scale-up. The pages already rank; they're just thin and text-heavy. Step 1 deepens the content, adds visuals and FAQs, and captures the high-volume ICON cluster the site already ranks #3 for.

  • Deepen every treatment page — expand thin content with procedure detail, candidacy, recovery & cost so #4–6 terms climb into the top 3.
  • Own the ICON cluster. You already rank #3 for the main term — build out the ICON content (white-spot, resin infiltration variations) to capture the 8+ page-2/3 terms worth thousands of searches.
  • Add visuals throughout — real doctor/team/office photos and cosmetic before-and-afters to break up the text and lift trust & conversion.
  • Add FAQs + FAQ schema to every treatment page — patient-language answers built for SEO and AI search.
  • Add the missing pages — a dedicated dental-implants page (and fix the cosmetic page mis-ranking in Elk Grove Village, IL).
  • Disavow the toxic links (~63% of referring domains at authority ≤2), then build quality local links.

Why this matters: the hard part is done — this site already ranks. Deeper content, visuals and FAQs on top of that base is the fastest, highest-ROI path to more traffic and new patients.
