Issues Ranked by Impact

The 12 issues, in priority order

Each issue includes evidence from the audit crawl and a recommended fix. The top five Critical issues are platform-rooted — they're the reason a 25-year practice with 422 5.0★ reviews and 1,204 keyword rankings only draws 176 monthly visits.

📸
Critical Photography & Trust Issue #01

Every treatment page uses generic stock photography — zero real practice images

Treatment pages across the site — /san-diego-ca/cosmetic-dentist/, /dental-veneers-and-dental-laminates/, /dental-bridges/, /full-mouth-reconstruction/, /composite-fillings/, /teeth-whitening/, /snap-on-smile/ and the rest — carry the DocGenius stock image set: generic patients, generic teeth, generic operatories. Patients researching a $5,000–$50,000 procedure want to see this doctor, this office, this team, and these results. Stock photos signal "templated chain site," not "established 25-year San Diego practice."

📌 Evidence

Every URL under /san-diego-ca/[service]/ uses identical-style stock photography. The /smile-gallery/ page exists with real before/after shots but isn't threaded into the treatment pages where prospective patients evaluate results.

✓ The fix

Schedule an in-office photography day: Dr. Yassi and team headshots, operatory and reception, technology in use (CEREC, 3D CBCT, digital impressions, same-day crowns), real before/after smile gallery shots threaded into cosmetic and veneer pages, and patient photography (with consent). Replace the DocGenius stock set on every treatment page.

🌐
Critical Platform & URL Architecture Issue #02

DocGenius template ID exposed at /dental-practice-sp0036/

The platform's internal template ID is publicly indexed at /dental-practice-sp0036/ — a clear "templated platform" fingerprint visible to both Google and to savvy patients. Every treatment URL follows the same /san-diego-ca/[service]/ pattern, which is the standard DocGenius URL scheme used across hundreds of dental practices on the same platform.

📌 Evidence

/dental-practice-sp0036/ · /find-your-local-dental-center/ · /san-diego-ca/dental-office/ · /san-diego-ca/dental-services/ — generic templated URLs and titles like "Take Care Of Your Smile" indexed and crawlable.

✓ The fix

Short-term: noindex and 301-redirect /dental-practice-sp0036/ and the generic /find-your-local-dental-center/ to the homepage. Long-term: rebuild on a modern platform with practice-specific URLs (/cosmetic-dentistry/, /dental-implants/) instead of the templated /san-diego-ca/[service]/ structure.

🤖
Critical No Schema Issue #03

Zero schema markup deployed — invisible to AI Overviews and rich results

No LocalBusiness, MedicalProcedure, FAQPage, AggregateRating, Person, or Review schema is detectable. Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Copilot all read schema to surface practice information, doctor credentials, ratings, and procedure data. The 422 Google reviews at 5.0★, the 25-year history, Dr. Yassi's credentials, and the procedure list are all hidden from that layer — the single biggest asset the practice has is invisible where it matters most.

✓ The fix

Deploy LocalBusiness + AggregateRating on the homepage (pulling the 422-review 5.0★ rating), MedicalProcedure + FAQPage on every treatment page, Person schema for Dr. Yassi, and Review schema on the testimonials page.

📊
Critical Page-2 Rankings Issue #04

1,204 ranking keywords (more than 2× the next-largest "Mission Valley" competitor) but only 176 monthly visits. Cosmetic and emergency dentistry pages rank for 24 and 35 keywords respectively but together produce 3 visits/month.

⭐
Critical Reputation Not Surfaced Issue #05

1,000+ reviews at 5.0★ across three platforms — and the website barely shows it

The reputation is exceptional: 422 Google reviews · 482 Birdeye · 110 Yelp — all at 5.0★. Most San Diego practices peak in the 100–300 review range. The website has a /reviews/ page but no above-the-fold review carousel, no AggregateRating schema, no embedded testimonials on treatment pages, no "★ 5.0 — 422 reviews" social-proof bar in the header. The single strongest local-search asset the practice owns is the one its website surfaces least.

✓ The fix

Above-the-fold star rating + review count bar on every page. Embedded testimonial carousel on the homepage. Per-procedure testimonials on cosmetic, implants, veneers, and full-mouth reconstruction pages. AggregateRating schema across LocalBusiness markup. Trust badges (Established 2000 · 422 5.0★ · 25 years in Mission Valley) in the first viewport.

📄
High Thin Templated Content Issue #06

Treatment-page copy is generic DocGenius templating — not practice-specific

The /san-diego-ca/[service]/ pages carry the standard DocGenius copy templates: short, generic, and substitutable for any practice. No mention of Dr. Yassi's procedure experience, no San Diego patient examples, no embedded FAQs, no references to the practice's CEREC same-day crown technology or 3D CBCT scanning, no cost guidance, no financing notes. The same templated copy that produces page-2 rankings instead of page-1 rankings.

✓ The fix

Rewrite each treatment page with 1,000–1,500 words of practice-specific content: Dr. Yassi's procedure experience, San Diego patient examples (with consent), 5–8 procedure FAQs, embedded testimonials, before/after gallery integration, cost ranges and financing context, and the practice's technology stack (CEREC, 3D CBCT, digital impressions, same-day crowns).

📱
High Conversion Design Issue #07

No sticky CTAs, no above-fold trust signals, weak mobile conversion path

Above the fold: the 422 5.0★ rating, the 25-year history, Dr. Yassi's name, and the consult-booking CTA aren't surfaced in the first viewport on treatment pages. Mobile UX: no persistent sticky phone-number bar or "Book Online" bar pinned to the viewport bottom — the most reliable conversion lever on a dental site. CTAs: default DocGenius button styling; no per-procedure consult-request paths.

✓ The fix

Sticky phone-number / "Book Consult" bar pinned to mobile viewport bottom. Above-the-fold "Book a Consultation" CTA on every page. Trust signals (★ 5.0 · 422 reviews · 25 years in Mission Valley · Dr. Yassi) in the first viewport. Per-procedure consult-request forms (cosmetic / implants / full-mouth / emergency) with relevant fields rather than one generic form.

🖼️
High Trust Asset Not Threaded Issue #09

Smile Gallery exists — but isn't surfaced on the pages where it would convert

The site has a /smile-gallery/ before/after page — exactly the kind of trust signal that converts cosmetic and implant patients. Currently it sits as a single linked page rather than being threaded into the cosmetic dentist, veneers, full-mouth reconstruction, snap-on smile, and bonding pages where prospective patients are actively evaluating results.

✓ The fix

Per-treatment before/after thumbnail grids embedded directly on cosmetic, veneers, full-mouth reconstruction, and snap-on smile pages — linked through to the full /smile-gallery/. Add Dr. Yassi-attributed case studies (with consent) showing actual treatment outcomes from this practice.

🔍
Medium Commercial Page Weakness Issue #10

"Find a Dentist" page outperforms every commercial treatment page

/about/find-a-dentist/ draws 26% of total traffic — more than any treatment page on the site. Traffic is driven by brand and find-a-dentist intent, not procedure evaluation. The dedicated commercial pages that should be the conversion engine (cosmetic dentist, dental implants, veneers, full-mouth reconstruction, emergency) produce 1–16 visits each. The pages exist; they aren't earning the rankings they should.

✓ The fix

Internal-linking rebuild: descriptive anchor-text links from /about/find-a-dentist/ and the homepage into the cosmetic, implants, emergency, and full-mouth treatment pages. Per-treatment hub structure where Dr. Yassi's bio routes into procedure-specific pages with consult CTAs.

☎️
Medium NAP Consistency Issue #11

Two phone numbers in circulation across the practice's listings

The website displays (619) 805-4248 as the primary number, but external listings (patientconnect365 and several directory sites) still carry the older (619) 574-1810. NAP (Name · Address · Phone) inconsistency across citations weakens the local-search signal that the map pack depends on.

✓ The fix

Citation audit — Yelp, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, BBB, San Diego County Dental Society, Birdeye, patientconnect365, and dental directories. Ensure 2333 Camino del Rio S Suite 140 and the current primary phone number are consistent everywhere. Either retire the legacy number or set up a permanent forward.

📷
Medium Visual Assets on Directories Issue #12

Only 20 photos on Yelp — low visual asset count across directories

Yelp shows 20 photos for the practice — low for a 25-year-old office with 110 Yelp reviews. By comparison, Smile Mission Valley Dental Center has 31 photos with fewer reviews. Visual asset density on Yelp and Google Business Profile correlates with directory click-through and conversion on mobile.

✓ The fix

Use the in-office photo day from Issue #01 to refresh both the website photography and the Yelp / GBP photo libraries simultaneously. Target 40+ photos each: office interior, team, reception, operatory, technology in use, and a curated before/after subset.

Strategic Positioning

Lead with the doctor to win the brand-resolution moment

"Mission Valley Dental Arts" lives next door to three other Mission Valley-branded dental practices. A patient on the map pack can't easily tell them apart in 3 seconds. The fastest disambiguation is leading the brand with Dr. Yassi's name across the website, GBP, and citations.

Recommended brand treatment

From "Mission Valley Dental Arts" to "Dr. Yassi · Mission Valley Dental Arts"

Leading the brand with Dr. Yassi differentiates the practice from Mission Valley Dentists, Smile Mission Valley Dental Center, and Dentists of Mission Valley — three competitors all using the geographic phrase in their name. The change doesn't require a legal rename — it's about consistent anchor text in the website header, page titles, GBP, schema, citations, and partnership mentions.
