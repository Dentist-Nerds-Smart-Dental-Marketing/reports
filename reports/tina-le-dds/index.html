Our Game Plan

How Dentist Nerds Fixes This

This practice does not need a rebuild. It needs the duplicate pages merged, dental schema added, and the Google profile fixed — then the momentum it already has starts converting instead of leaking.

01
The Foundation
Consolidate the duplicates, then add the schema.

What we'd do in Step 1

Most audits open with "rebuild the site." Not this one. This site has 63 pages, 192 ranking keywords and four terms already in the top 3 — the content work has largely been done. Two things are stopping it from compounding: it publishes several pages chasing the same keyword, so Google never knows which to rank, and it carries no dental schema at all, so AI engines have nothing to read.

  • Merge the duplicate clusters, one canonical page per treatment. Three crown pages become one. Two bridge pages become one. Two family-dentistry pages, two cosmetic pages — same. Redirect the rest into the survivor so the ranking strength stacks instead of splitting seven ways.
  • Stop the homepage cannibalizing the service pages. "Dental implants los altos" and "dental crowns los altos" currently rank via the homepage while the dedicated pages sit at #21 and lower. Internal links and on-page targeting should point each term at its own page.
  • Add the dental schema the site is missing entirely — Dentist, LocalBusiness, MedicalProcedure, AggregateRating, and FAQPage on the FAQ pages that already exist. Right now there is none of it, which is why AI engines have nothing to cite.
  • Optimize the pages that exist but do not rank. "Teeth whitening los altos" (70/mo), "emergency dentist los altos" (70/mo) and "invisalign los altos" (90/mo) all have pages already — none of them ranks.
  • Point the Google listing at https. The profile currently links to the http:// version of the site.
  • Rewrite titles and meta descriptions across the merged pages so each one targets a single Los Altos term cleanly.

Why this matters: keywords nearly quadrupled since May while traffic only doubled, and last month keywords rose again while traffic slipped. That gap is the duplicates at work. Fix the split and the growth already underway starts landing on page one instead of page three.
