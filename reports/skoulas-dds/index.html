What Can We Do to Help?

01

Map every cosmetic keyword to one specific URL. Veneers, cosmetic, smile design, Invisalign — currently all compete from the homepage. Set canonicals and internal links.

Launch Review Engine

Press release distribution. Award badge on homepage hero. Press & awards page with backlinks. Pitch follow-up coverage to SF Chronicle, SF Gate, Refinery29, Goop, Vogue (high-DA cosmetic targets).

⚠️Website Architecture Issues

Service pages exist under /services/ but homepage targets all 64+ keywords. Most cosmetic service URLs (veneers, composite-dental-veneers, cosmetic-dentistry) exist but fail to rank — homepage outranks them on their own keywords.

⚠️Helpful Content Update Impact

Traffic dropped 50% between Oct 2023 and Jan 2025 — the textbook signature of a Google Helpful Content Update penalty. Several blog posts read like generic AI-templated content. Quality content investment is required to recover fully.

⚠️Mobile & Speed

Full mobile/Core Web Vitals audit recommended. Long-running WordPress installation likely accumulating bloat — outdated images, plugins, and legacy code potentially affecting Core Web Vitals on mobile.

🔗Internal Linking

No visible internal linking strategy. Service pages don't cross-link. Blog posts ("Can Porcelain Veneers Stain", "Zirconia Bridge", "Biolase Dentistry") do get traffic but don't link to the corresponding service pages — wasting their link equity. The homepage doesn't funnel authority to the most important treatment pages, which is why the homepage is doing 92% of the work.
