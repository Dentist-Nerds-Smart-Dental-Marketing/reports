What we'd do in Step 1

No rebuild is needed — this is the best-built site the practice has. Step 1 lets Google see it, and positions it as the home for Dr. Hanookai's specialty.

  • Lift the noindex today. Untick “Discourage search engines” in WordPress, add canonical tags, and submit the sitemap in Search Console.
  • Disavow the spam. Remove the ~1,800 spam domains from the equation, keeping the genuine 2023–2024 links.
  • Give the profile its own lane. Make “Periodontist” the primary category, with implants secondary, and add (818) 788-8787 as an additional phone.
  • Become the specialist hub. Redirect the old delaramhanookai.com pages here, and have Southland's periodontics pages link to this site.
  • Grow the reviews. Point periodontal and implant patients to Dr. Hanookai's profile — 24 reviews against Southland's 277.
  • Get under 2.5 seconds. Move the 860 KB of inline CSS into a cached stylesheet and serve next-gen images.

Why this matters: every page is already written for exactly the searches Dr. Hanookai wants to win. The moment Google is allowed in, that work starts counting.
