Biggest Priorities

Top 3 Weaknesses

The three issues doing the most damage right now — fix these first and everything else compounds faster.

01

The site lost 78% of its keywords

In June 2023 this domain ranked for 523 keywords. By February 2024 it was 144, and it bottomed at 37 in mid-2025. Today: 113. Whatever happened to the site in early 2024 took four fifths of its search footprint with it, and it has never been rebuilt.

Critical
02

Not one line of schema, anywhere

We read the markup on the homepage, services, crowns, implants, veneers and about pages. There is no structured data at all — not even the generic WebPage tags most sites ship by default. No Dentist, no LocalBusiness, no FAQ, no reviews. Google and every AI engine are reading this site blind.

Critical
03

Seven pages against a 6,000-search market

The sitemap lists seven pages. San Jose searches for a dentist roughly 5,980 times a month, including "dental implants san jose" at 1,300/mo and $27.09 a click. The implants page is 456 words; the crowns page is 212. There is nothing built to compete for any of it.

Critical
Other issues we found
  • The veneers page URL is misspelled — /veeners-san-jose/ — and a second /veneers/ page exists alongside it
  • /crowns/, /braces/ and /veneers/ are live but missing from the sitemap entirely
  • The WordPress default "Sample Page" is still published at /sample-page/
  • The crowns page title tag is literally the word "crowns" — lowercase, no city, no practice name
  • 5.2% of traffic still lands on the insecure http:// version of the site
  • No dedicated contact or booking page — both menu items are anchors on the homepage
  • Four to five URLs compete for each branded term, splitting their own signal

01
The Foundation
Rebuild the site, then win back the 410 lost keywords.

What we'd do in Step 1

This is the heaviest lift — and the precondition for everything that comes next. The practice already proved it can rank for 523 keywords; the site that earned them no longer exists in any usable form. We rebuild it properly, then rebuild the footprint on top of a foundation that can hold it.

  • Rebuild the website. Seven indexed pages cannot compete in a market of 5,980 monthly searches. A full treatment library — implants, veneers, crowns, Invisalign, emergency, whitening, dentures, root canal — each a real page written for San Jose.
  • Fix the URL and crawl problems on day one. The veneers page is spelled /veeners-san-jose/; a duplicate /veneers/ sits beside it; /crowns/, /braces/ and /veneers/ are missing from the sitemap; the WordPress "Sample Page" is still published; and 5.2% of traffic still arrives over insecure http.
  • Add schema markup, because right now there is none at all — Dentist, LocalBusiness, MedicalProcedure, FAQPage and AggregateRating on every page. This site currently gives Google and the AI engines nothing structured to read.
  • Give the practice a real contact and booking page. Both menu items are anchors on the homepage today, so nothing can rank for them and nothing captures booking intent on its own URL.
  • Protect the brand asset while rebuilding. Six name variations at #1 carry 94.7% of current traffic — and four to five URLs compete for each one. Consolidate those without losing the rankings that pay the bills today.
  • Rewrite every title and meta description. The crowns page title is currently the single lowercase word "crowns."

Why this matters: 94.7% of the traffic depends on patients already knowing the name — and a differently-owned practice at blossomdentist.com is chasing the same Blossom Hill searches with more than twice the content. The brand is a real asset, but it is the only one, and it is not defended.
