Our Game Plan

How Dentist Nerds Fixes This

We don't believe in flashy launches that flame out. The plan starts with a strong foundation, then layers ongoing engines on top — each one compounding the last.

⚡ Start Here
01
The Foundation
Rebuild the website on an accessible, SEO-ready foundation.

What we'd do in Step 1

This is the heaviest lift — and the precondition for everything that comes next. We rebuild the website with a strong technical, accessibility and on-page SEO foundation. Without this layer in place, the work in the engines below won't compound the way it should.

  • Fix ADA accessibility first. Proper color contrast, alt text on every image, labeled forms and accessible navigation — closing the compliance gap our review flagged.
  • Rebuild on a modern platform with clean visual design, premium positioning, and obvious book/call CTAs on every page.
  • Lock in a strong SEO structure. Proper heading hierarchy, semantic HTML, clean internal linking between service pages, Core Web Vitals optimized from day one.
  • Add FAQ schema to every treatment page — the FAQs are already written in patient language; they just need FAQPage markup so Google and AI engines can use them.
  • Schema markup on every page — Dentist, Physician, FAQPage, MedicalProcedure, AggregateRating.
  • Repoint & rewrite thin pages so "la habra dentist", "invisalign la habra" and similar terms land on the right service page instead of Contact — and can actually rank.

Why this matters: a strong foundation is what every other channel — reviews, backlinks, content, AI search — needs to actually compound. Skipping it means the rest of the work leaks instead of stacks.
