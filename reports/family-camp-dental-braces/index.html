Our Game Plan

How Dentist Nerds Fixes This

We don't believe in flashy launches that flame out. The plan starts with a strong foundation, then layers ongoing engines on top — each one compounding the last.

⚡ Start Here
01
The Foundation
Give every treatment its own page & tighten the brand.

What we'd do in Step 1

The rankings are already there for braces and brand terms — the ceiling is structure. Step 1 breaks the single scrolling page into dedicated, deeper treatment pages, adds FAQs, and dials the brand back from generic-colorful to a distinct, premium family + orthodontics look.

  • Give every treatment its own page. Break the one scrolling page into dedicated, indexable pages (pediatric, braces/ortho, family dentistry, implants, affordability) with clean URLs and internal links.
  • Deepen the content on each page — procedure detail, candidacy, what-to-expect, and cost so each service can actually rank.
  • Own "braces payment plan" & affordability (2,400 searches/mo, currently #30) with a strong dedicated page — a natural fit for this practice.
  • Add FAQs + FAQ schema to every page — patient-language answers built for SEO and AI search.
  • Refine the brand. Pull back the over-colorful, generic pediatric look toward a distinct, premium family + orthodontics identity that builds parent trust.
  • Light backlink cleanup of the ~59% low-authority domains, then keep building on an already-healthy profile.

Why this matters: the practice already ranks #1 for braces — it just has one page doing all the work. Give each service its own home and the traffic (and new patients) compounds fast.
