Executive Summary · Your Questions, Answered

Key Findings

#1 Weakness — A thin, branded-only site with no money pages
The single biggest issue: the website is just four pages and ranks for no treatment at all, so it captures none of the high-value "implants / Invisalign / veneers" demand. With no content engine, the 2025 traffic surge had nothing to stand on and gave back 54% of its peak. Fixing this — real treatment pages with schema — is the highest-leverage move available.
Missing doctor information — fix this first (trust & E-E-A-T gap)
The site doesn't surface clear information about the dentist — Dr. Arman Mirai's bio, credentials, and experience aren't presented prominently, and there's no Person / Dentist schema tying the practice to a named provider. For a healthcare (YMYL) site, Google weighs this heavily for trust, and AI assistants need a clearly identified, credentialed provider before they'll recommend one. It's one of the most impactful — and fastest — fixes available.
Web traffic peaked at 125/mo and has fallen to ~57
Organic visits grew from 2 (Apr 2025) to a peak of 125 (Sep–Oct 2025), then declined steadily to 35 in April 2026 before recovering slightly to 57. The site clearly can rank — it just isn't being fed the pages and links to hold ground.
All traffic enters one page — the homepage
The homepage carries 100% of organic visits and 66 of the site's keywords. The /our-services, /about and /contact-us pages rank only on page 2–10 (positions 40–96) and bring zero traffic. There is no second front door into the site.
Backlink profile is light — only 6 "followed" links
35 referring domains and 80 backlinks sounds reasonable, but just 6 are dofollow (the kind that pass ranking authority); the other 74 are nofollow. Authority Score sits at 8/100. Competitors out-rank the site largely on this gap.
What's working — a #2 brand ranking and a 4.8★ reputation
The site ranks #2 for "anaheim hills dentist" (480 searches/mo) and top-7 for five more local terms — it owns its core neighborhood brand, and patients rate the practice highly (detailed in the GBP scorecard below). These are real assets to build on, not start from scratch.
