Executive Summary

Key Findings

100% of Traffic Is Branded — No Discovery Engine
All 32 monthly visits come from a single branded search, "next smile and implant" (position 1). Generic, new-patient keywords like "dental implants thousand oaks" (pos 48), "cosmetic dentistry thousand oaks" (pos 45), and "porcelain veneers thousand oaks" (pos 29) drive no traffic. The practice is found by people who already know it — not discovered by new patients.
Only 38 Keywords Ranking — Should Be 400+
A Thousand Oaks practice competing across dental implants, All-on-X, full-mouth reconstruction, veneers, cosmetic and general dentistry should rank for hundreds of keywords. It ranks for 38 — and that number has fallen from 60 in February 2026. Overall organic traffic is very low and the keyword footprint is shrinking, not growing.
Treatment Pages Have No FAQ Content — Hurting AI Visibility
The treatment pages carry no FAQ sections and no FAQ/Service schema. This is one of the biggest reasons the practice is invisible to AI search — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews rely on structured Q&A content to understand, trust and cite a dental practice. Without it, these pages stay buried on page 3–10.
Keyword Cannibalization & Missing Treatment Pages
Only a handful of treatment pages are indexed, and several compete against themselves — "ceramic dental implants thousand oaks" ranks with 4 different URLs (pos 28, 33, 45, 83), splitting authority instead of concentrating it. Meanwhile there are no dedicated pages for Invisalign/orthodontics, dentures, root canals, emergency dentistry, teeth whitening, crowns & bridges, or IV sedation. Patients searching those treatments have nothing optimized to land on.
Backlink Profile Needs Evaluation — Almost No Link Equity
There are 736 backlinks across 108 referring domains, but only 8 are "follow" links — the other 735 are nofollow and pass essentially no ranking authority. The profile needs a full evaluation and a deliberate, high-authority link-building plan so the practice actually accrues the authority it needs to rank.
