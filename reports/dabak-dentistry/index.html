Executive Summary · Your Questions, Answered

Key Findings

#1 Weakness — Google knows the doctor, not the practice
"Apurva dabak dds" (40/mo) ranks #1 and delivers 96.9% of all organic traffic. Every term that describes what the practice does is buried: "dentist redwood city" (260/mo) at #44, "dentist redwood city ca" (260/mo) at #41, "redwood city dentist" (140/mo) at #39, "dentist in redwood city" (140/mo) at #41. A patient who already knows Dr. Dabak's name finds the site instantly. A patient looking for a dentist in Redwood City never sees it.
0% share of the Google Map — and a competitor proves it isn't the reviews
A live grid scan for "dentist redwood city" from 25 points across a 10-mile radius puts Dabak Dentistry at an average map rank of #21.0, in the top three at zero of 25 points, with 0% share of local voice. The comparison that matters: Toral K Patel, DDS holds 5.0★ across 39 reviews — one fewer than this practice — and averages rank #5.8 with 8% share. Same rating, same review count, entirely different visibility. What separates them is everything else in this report.
What's working — the technical foundation is genuinely good
We checked the live markup: FAQPage schema is present on the home, about and services pages; AggregateRating is on the home page; Dentist, MedicalProcedure, Person and City schema are all correctly implemented. Most practices we audit are missing all of this. The site is not badly built — it is simply too small, and undermined by one configuration error.
Twelve pages total — and not one treatment page
The sitemap holds 12 URLs: home, about, services, contact, blog, finances, privacy, accessibility and four blog posts. Seven distinct treatments — general dentistry, implants, whitening, veneers, Invisalign, aesthetic dentistry and emergency care — are compressed into a single 1,069-word /services page, about 150 words each. We tested the obvious URLs: /dental-implants, /veneers, /invisalign, /emergency-dentist and /teeth-whitening all return 404.
A canonical conflict is splitting the site against itself
Requesting dabakdentistry.com returns a 307 redirect to the www version — but every page's canonical tag declares the non-www address. We confirmed the contradiction on all six main pages. Google receives two conflicting instructions about where each page really lives, and the damage is measurable: the brand term ranks with five different URLs at once (#1, #7, #17, #76, #89), and both dabakdentistry.com/ and www.dabakdentistry.com/ appear as separate ranking pages.
A blog post is doing the emergency page's job
The site's best non-branded rankings all belong to one article, /blog/signs-you-need-dentist-redwood-city: #11 for "same day dental appointments redwood city," #23 for "emergency dentist redwood city" (90/mo) and #23 for "redwood city emergency dentist." The meta description promises "same-day emergency care," but there is no emergency page for that promise to land on. A proper service page would outrank the blog post that is currently standing in for it.
165 referring domains, Authority Score 7
372 backlinks from 165 domains ought to buy more than 7/100. That gap is the signature of directory-grade links that pass little real strength. On a site this young — first indexed in June 2026 — authority is the lever that decides whether new treatment pages land on page one or on page four alongside everything else.
The Google listing and the website use different names
The Google Business Profile is titled "Apurva Dabak DDS." The website brands itself "Dabak Dentistry." That mismatch is very likely why the only term this practice owns is the doctor's personal name — it is the entity Google has learned. The profile is otherwise in good shape: claimed, 5.0★ across 40 reviews, and correctly linked to the website. It runs a single category, "Dentist," with no secondaries.

Triage Center

Usability & Technical Issues

307
Canonical conflict — the site contradicts itself on every page
The server 307-redirects dabakdentistry.com to www.dabakdentistry.com, but each page's canonical tag declares the non-www address. Verified on home, about, services, contact, blog and finances. Pick one host, redirect to it, and point every canonical at the same one. This is a config change measured in minutes and it is the highest-leverage technical fix on the site.
5
Brand-term cannibalization — five URLs chasing one keyword
"Apurva dabak dds" ranks five separate times: the homepage at #1, /about at #7, /blog at #17, /contact at #76 and /services at #89. Google is splitting the signal across pages that were never meant to compete. A direct consequence of the canonical conflict above, and it resolves with it.
7→1
Seven treatments compressed into one page
General dentistry, implants, whitening, veneers, Invisalign, aesthetic dentistry and emergency care share a single 1,069-word /services page — roughly 150 words per treatment. No single treatment has enough depth to outrank a competitor's dedicated page, and there is no URL for Google to associate with "dental implants redwood city" or any other treatment term.
404
The obvious treatment URLs don't exist
We tested them directly: /dental-implants, /veneers, /invisalign, /emergency-dentist and /teeth-whitening all return 404. Any inbound link, directory listing or AI citation pointing at a conventional treatment URL lands on an error page rather than a booking opportunity.
ID
Two different business names in play
Google Business Profile: "Apurva Dabak DDS." Website: "Dabak Dentistry." Domain: dabakdentistry.com. Mixed naming splits brand-search signal and weakens entity recognition for both Google and AI assistants — and it is the likeliest reason the personal name is the only term that ranks. Choose one official name and apply it identically to the site, the schema, the GBP and every directory.
CTA
No online booking on the site
We found no booking widget or appointment-request flow in the page markup — every appointment starts with a phone call. The patient comparing practices at 9pm has no way to act, and there is no measurement of how many were lost at that moment.
