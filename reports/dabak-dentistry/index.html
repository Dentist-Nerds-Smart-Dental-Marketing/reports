Our Game Plan

How Dentist Nerds Fixes This

This practice does not need a rebuild — the schema, markup and design are already right, which is rarer than it sounds. It needs the two things it's missing: a clean signal, and pages to rank. Step 1 delivers both; the engines below compound on top.

⚡ Start Here
01
The Foundation
Fix the signal, then build the treatment pages.

What we'd do in Step 1

Most audits open with "rebuild the site." Not this one. The foundation here is genuinely good — FAQPage, AggregateRating, MedicalProcedure and Dentist schema are all correctly implemented, and the design is clean. Two things are holding it back, and both are fixable without touching the design: a configuration error that makes the site argue with itself, and the absence of any page for the treatments the practice actually performs.

  • Resolve the canonical conflict first. Pick one host, redirect everything to it, and point every canonical tag at the same address. This stops Google receiving contradictory instructions on all 12 pages and ends the five-URL pile-up on the brand term. Minutes of work — and every other item compounds on top of it.
  • Split /services into real treatment pages. Dental implants (210/mo, $30.88 a click), teeth whitening (110), cosmetic dentistry (110), crowns (110), Invisalign (90), veneers (90), root canal (90) and dentures (90) — each a page written for Redwood City, not 150 words inside a shared one.
  • Give the emergency rankings a page to live on. A blog post is currently the site's best non-branded asset (#11, #23, #23). The site advertises same-day emergency care with no page behind the promise.
  • Extend the schema you already ship — FAQPage, MedicalProcedure and AggregateRating onto every new treatment page, so each one is citable by ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and AI Overviews from day one.
  • Unify the practice name. Google lists "Apurva Dabak DDS," the site says "Dabak Dentistry." That split is very likely why the doctor's personal name is the only term this practice owns.
  • Add online booking. Every appointment currently starts with a phone call — the patient comparing practices at 9pm has no way to act.

Why this matters: right now Google knows Dr. Dabak but not the practice — 96.9% of all traffic is one search for the doctor's own name, and the site holds 0% of the local map. Step 1 is what turns a well-built 12-page site into something that can actually be found by a patient who doesn't already know the name.
