ISSUE 1 Critical · Mobile Experience

The website is not mobile-friendly — and over 60% of dental patients search on their phones.

In 2026, a non-mobile-friendly dental website is effectively invisible to the majority of prospective patients. Most people searching "dentist near me" or looking up a specific practice are doing it on their smartphone. If the website doesn't render properly on mobile — text is too small, buttons are hard to tap, content overflows the screen — patients bounce immediately and call the next practice on the list.

Beyond user experience, Google uses mobile-first indexing: it primarily uses the mobile version of a page to determine search rankings. A site that isn't mobile-friendly is directly penalized in search results, compounding the already-low traffic of 35 visits per month.

✓ The Fix

The new website will be built mobile-first from the ground up — fully responsive, fast-loading on all screen sizes, and optimized for the thumb-tap interactions patients use on their phones. This is a foundational requirement of the rebuild, not an afterthought.

ISSUE 2 Critical · Trust & Conversion

An outdated design full of stock imagery signals to patients that this practice isn't keeping up — even though it clearly is.

Stock photography is a trust killer. When patients see generic smiling models and generic dental stock photos, they subconsciously register that the practice didn't invest in showing them who they actually are. It reads as impersonal — the opposite of what dental patients need to feel before choosing a provider.

Combine that with an outdated site design and the visual impression is that the practice is behind the times. This is especially damaging for a practice with 370 five-star reviews: patients read those reviews, get excited, visit the website, and the visual experience deflates the enthusiasm those reviews created.

✓ The Fix

We will coordinate or guide a professional photo shoot to capture real imagery of the practice — the office, the team, the environment. All stock photography will be replaced with authentic visuals that reflect who Shinto & Kato actually is. Real photos convert dramatically better than stock imagery because they answer the question patients are actually asking: what will it feel like to be a patient here?

ISSUE 3 Critical · Content Architecture

Only two treatment pages in the main navigation — Google can't rank what it can't find, and patients can't book services they don't see.

A dental practice typically offers 10–20 distinct services: cleanings, fillings, whitening, veneers, implants, Invisalign, root canals, extractions, emergency care, and more. Each of these should have its own dedicated page with substantive content. Each page is a separate opportunity to rank in Google when a patient searches for that specific service.

With only two treatment pages in the navigation, the site has effectively told Google it offers two things. Every patient searching for any other service the practice provides — even services they definitely offer — will never find them organically. This is a direct cause of the 35 visits/month figure and the 214-keyword ceiling.

✓ The Fix

The new website will be architected with a complete treatment page structure — every service the practice offers gets its own dedicated, content-rich page with 500–1,000+ words, patient-focused copy, and FAQ sections. This is the most direct path from 214 keywords to 600+ keywords, and from 35 monthly visits to meaningful new-patient traffic.

ISSUE 4 High · SEO Visibility

214 ranking keywords is far below the 500–600 minimum benchmark — the site is missing most patient discovery searches.

The 214-keyword count isn't just a number — it's a direct reflection of how much of the patient search landscape this website is currently visible in. A healthy dental website ranks for 500–600 keywords at minimum; established practices with strong content rank for 1,000–2,000+. At 214, the site is participating in less than half the relevant searches that could be driving new patients.

This isn't about gaming Google — it's about having enough content for Google to understand what services the practice offers and where it's located. Every keyword gap is a patient who searched for something the practice offers, didn't find them, and booked with a competitor instead.

✓ The Fix

The combination of a complete treatment page structure, strong local SEO signals (consistent NAP, location pages, Google Business Profile alignment), and FAQ content for AI search will organically expand the keyword footprint. The target is 600+ keywords within 6 months of the new site launch and 1,000+ within a year.

ISSUE 5 High · AI Search Readiness

No FAQ content means zero presence in AI-powered search — ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overview, and Perplexity.

Search behavior is changing rapidly. A growing share of dental patients now start their research by asking an AI assistant — "who is the best dentist near me," "what does a dental implant procedure involve," "does Shinto and Kato do Invisalign." These AI tools pull answers from structured content on websites: specifically, well-written FAQ sections and clear service descriptions.

Without FAQ content on service pages, the practice is completely absent from this growing search channel. The new site will be built with AI visibility as a first-class requirement — not a future consideration.

✓ The Fix

Every major treatment page in the new site will include a structured FAQ section with 5–8 questions patients commonly ask about that service, formatted with FAQ schema markup. This positions the site to be cited in Google AI Overview results, ChatGPT responses, and other AI-powered search tools — a visibility channel that's entirely untapped today.

Monthly Local Listings Cleanup

Inconsistent business listings across Google, Yelp, Apple Maps, Bing, and other directories directly suppress local search rankings. Every month, we audit and correct listing inaccuracies to ensure the practice information is consistent, up-to-date, and sending the right signals to Google.

Four Monthly SEO Blocks

Each month, four focused content and SEO additions are published to the site — new service pages, local landing pages, FAQ sections, or supporting content. These compound over time, steadily growing the keyword footprint and visibility in local search results.

Monthly Backlinks & Authority Building

Backlinks — other websites linking to yours — are one of the strongest signals Google uses to determine how authoritative and trustworthy a site is. Each month, we build high-quality, relevant backlinks through dental directories, local business citations, and industry publications. Over time, this growing authority directly improves rankings in traditional search and increases the likelihood of being cited by AI tools like Google AI Overview, ChatGPT, and Perplexity when patients ask questions about dental care in your area.

Monthly Reports & Continuous Improvements

Every month you'll receive a clear, readable report showing keyword growth, traffic trends, listing status, and what was done. No jargon, no black box. You'll always know exactly what's happening with the practice's online presence and where the growth is coming from.
