Section 02 — Website Audit

Why the Current Website Cannot Work

The website has several fundamental problems that go beyond SEO. Even if a patient did find the site, the experience would not convert them into a booked appointment. These issues are structural — they cannot be patched. They require a new foundation.

Critical Issue

Dental & Wellness Mixed on One Site

Combining two distinct services — dental and wellness — under one website confuses every visitor. A patient looking for a dentist doesn't know if they've landed at the right place. A patient seeking wellness services feels the same. Confused visitors don't book appointments. Search engines also struggle to categorize a site that tries to serve two different audiences, which directly suppresses rankings for both.

Critical Issue

Virtually No Written Content

Google ranks pages based on content — words that answer patient questions and signal expertise. The current website has minimal text. There are no service descriptions, no treatment explanations, no FAQs, no blog. Without content, there is nothing for Google to index and nothing to build rankings from. The site is essentially a digital business card, not a patient acquisition tool.

Critical Issue

Script Fonts & Image Overlays Block Readability

The use of decorative script fonts and text placed over images makes the site extremely difficult to read — especially on mobile. For patients who are older or have any visual impairment, this is an immediate barrier. Readability directly affects both conversion (whether someone stays and books) and accessibility compliance. It also signals poor quality to new patients comparing multiple providers.

🚫
No SEO architecture

No title tags, meta descriptions, H1 structure, internal linking, or schema markup. The site has no technical SEO foundation whatsoever. Search engines cannot understand what the business offers or who it serves.

🚫
No call-to-action or conversion path

There is no clear "Book Appointment," "Call Now," or contact prompt that guides a visitor toward becoming a patient. Anyone who does land on the site has no obvious next step — so they leave.

🚫
No /llms.txt — completely invisible to AI platforms

With no structured content and no AI guidance file, ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity have no way to discover, understand, or recommend this practice. AI-driven patient searches return zero results for RES Health Group.

Section 03 — Strategic Recommendation

One Website Is Holding Two Businesses Hostage

The single most important strategic decision is this: dental and wellness need to be separate websites. This isn't a preference — it is a requirement for either service to rank, convert, or grow online.

The Problem — One Mixed Site

Competing against yourself

  • Google can't categorize the site — dental or wellness?
  • Dental patients don't trust a site that's also promoting wellness services
  • Wellness patients feel the same in reverse
  • Every page competes with itself for relevance
  • No focused keyword strategy is possible
  • Two audiences = zero clear messaging for either
The Solution — Two Focused Websites

Each site built to win its own market

  • Dedicated dental site — ranks for dentist, implants, whitening, Invisalign
  • Dedicated wellness site — ranks for its own services and audience
  • Clear messaging for each patient type from the first second
  • Twice the SEO surface area, twice the ranking potential
  • Each site can have its own content strategy and blog
  • Visitors immediately know they're in the right place
Section 04 — AI Search Visibility

The AI Era Has Started. You're Not In It.

Patients are increasingly asking ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews, and Perplexity to recommend local dentists and wellness providers. These platforms pull answers from structured, content-rich websites. With zero content and no AI-readable structure, RES Health Group does not appear in any of these answers — today or in the near future without a complete rebuild.

AI Visibility Assessment

Three Reasons AI Platforms Cannot Recommend You

  • No content to read: AI platforms index and learn from written content — service descriptions, FAQs, blog posts. With minimal text on the site, there is nothing for AI to surface when a patient asks for a recommendation.
  • No /llms.txt: This file tells AI crawlers who you are and what you offer. Without it, even AI systems that do find the site cannot properly understand or represent your practice.
  • No structured data or schema: Search engines and AI platforms rely on structured markup to understand business type, location, services, and reviews. None of this is present on the current site.
