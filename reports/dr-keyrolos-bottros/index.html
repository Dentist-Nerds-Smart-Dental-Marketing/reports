Issues Ranked by Impact

The 12 issues, in priority order

Each issue includes evidence from the audit crawl and a recommended fix. Fixing the top five Critical issues alone — without any content or backlink work — should restore the November 2025 peak of 137 visits/month within 90 days.

🚫
Critical Crawler Access Issue #01

Cloudflare 429 Too Many Requests blocking 8+ critical pages

Multiple service URLs — /clear-aligners-and-orthodontics, /dental-emergency, /kids-dentistry, /general-dentistry, /home, /before-and-after, plus three blog posts — return 429 Too Many Requests to crawlers. Google cannot index pages it cannot reach.

📌 Evidence

Multiple service URLs return "429 Too Many Requests" when crawled. The legacy non-www domain also returns 403 Cloudflare challenges across the entire site.

✓ The fix

Adjust the Cloudflare bot-challenge / rate-limiting policy to whitelist verified Googlebot, Bingbot, and other major crawlers. Verify in Search Console that all service pages are now reachable.

🔀
Critical Duplicate Content Issue #02

Two parallel "About" pages indexed + draft "Team 1" page live

Both /about and /about-2 are indexed with similar content and no rel="canonical." A third page at /team is titled "Team 1" — clearly a development page that shipped to production. Branded "about us" queries don't resolve cleanly to either page.

✓ The fix

Pick one canonical About URL, 301-redirect the other to it. Replace the draft /team page with real team content or remove it from the sitemap.

📢
Critical Title-Tag Spam Issue #03

Every page title ends with "— JOLIET DENTIST NEAR ME" in all caps

Every indexed page on the site ends the title with the string "— JOLIET DENTIST NEAR ME" in all caps. This is a classic 2010s-era SEO pattern that Google now penalizes for keyword stuffing — and the all-caps treatment undermines click-through rate.

📌 Evidence

"JOLIET DENTIST NEAR ME | Experience Quality Dental Care — Book Now"
"Cosmetic Dentistry | Enhance Your Smile Today – Book Now — JOLIET DENTIST NEAR ME"
"Implant Dentistry | Schedule Your Implant Consultation Today — JOLIET DENTIST NEAR ME"

✓ The fix

Rewrite every page title in normal case using the pattern "[Service] in Joliet, IL · Haven Family Dental." Drop the all-caps suffix entirely. Keep titles under 60 characters.

🌐
Critical Domain Architecture Issue #04

Parallel legacy + new site structures both indexed

The legacy WordPress site at havenfamilydental.com (non-www) has 20+ URLs indexed in Google's crawl data — almost all returning 403 Cloudflare challenges. The new site at www.havenfamilydental.com uses different URL slugs. Old backlinks point at URLs that no longer resolve.

📌 Evidence

Legacy URLs still indexed: /about-us/, /dental-implants/, /contact-us/, /dental-emergency/, /invisalign-clear-aligners/, /services/, /smile-makeover-veneers/, /reviews/, /hello-world/. All return 403.

✓ The fix

Decommission the legacy WordPress install entirely. Set up explicit 301 redirects from every old URL to the matching new URL. Submit the legacy URLs for removal in Search Console.

🎨
Critical Design · Typography · UX Issue #05

Dated design, inconsistent typography, stock-photo heroes, weak mobile UX

Typography: inconsistent across pages — multiple font weights and families with no clear hierarchy. No typographic scale (h1 / h2 / h3 / body / small) consistently applied. Line height is tight where it should breathe.

Layout & imagery: the implant page uses a generic Unsplash stock photo. Buttons inherit default platform styling. Section spacing is uneven. Trust signals (Dr. Bottros's Creighton credential, reviews, Joliet location) aren't surfaced above the fold.

Mobile UX: the "Book Now" CTA isn't persistent or sticky. Phone-number tap-to-call isn't always visible. Forms aren't optimized for mobile input — this is where conversions leak most.

✓ The fix

Define a typography system pairing one warm serif headline font (Playfair Display, Lora, or Cormorant Garamond) with one clean sans-serif body font (Inter, Manrope, or DM Sans), consistently applied. Establish a 6-level type scale (44/32/22/16/14/12 px) with line-height 1.5–1.7. Replace all stock images with real practice photography. Build mobile-first: sticky phone-number / "Book Online" bar pinned to viewport bottom, above-the-fold CTAs, trust signals in the first viewport.

📄
High Thin Service Pages Issue #06

Service pages average ~170 words — no structure, FAQs, or testimonials

The implant page runs roughly 170 words — far below the ~1,200 words competing Joliet service pages carry. No HTML structure, no FAQs, no testimonials, no before/after gallery, no cost ranges, no schema. To rank in search and surface in AI results, every service page needs proper structure and new content from scratch. Cosmetic, kids, and general dentistry pages follow the same thin template.

✓ The fix

Rebuild each service page with 1,000–1,500 words of practice-specific content, 5–8 procedure FAQs, embedded testimonials from existing reviews, before/after gallery, cost guidance or financing notes, schema markup, and a prominent above-the-fold "Book a Consultation" CTA.

⏰
High Content Errors Issue #07

Visible content errors — typo'd hours, empty gallery, draft team page

The contact section on multiple pages shows hours as "Tues - Thursday 9PM-6PM" — should be 9AM. The before-and-after page is in the navigation but contains no images. The /team page is titled "Team 1."

✓ The fix

Manual content audit pass — correct all hours references, populate the before/after gallery with real cases (with patient consent), build the team page with actual headshots and bios.

🤖
High No Schema Issue #08

Zero schema markup deployed — invisible to AI Overviews and rich results

No LocalBusiness, MedicalProcedure, FAQPage, AggregateRating, Review, or Person schema is detectable on any page. AI search engines (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot) and traditional rich-result panels rely on schema to surface procedure data, ratings, and provider credentials.

✓ The fix

Deploy LocalBusiness + AggregateRating on the homepage, MedicalProcedure + FAQPage on each service page, Person schema for Dr. Bottros, and Review schema on the testimonials page.

⛓️
High Backlink Spam Issue #09

58 of 98 referring domains are spam-grade — Telegram-anchor link network

The backlink profile is contaminated. 17 referring domains link with anchor text "join our telegram darksidelinks"; 6 more use "our telegram chanel quarterlinks25." Across the full profile, 58 of 98 referring domains carry Authority Score 2 or lower. Google reads coordinated anchor-text patterns from low-quality domains as link-scheme participation.

📌 Evidence · backlink anchor data

17 domains · "join our telegram https://t.me/s/darksidelinks"
6 domains · "our telegram chanel https://t.me/s/quarterlinks25"
Suspicious TLDs: .monster (2), .space (4), .sbs (2), .art (2), .in (9), .cv (3)

✓ The fix

Full referring-domain audit · classify each as keep / monitor / disavow · submit a disavow file to Search Console for the 58+ spam-grade domains · run a parallel local-link program to replace toxic links with legitimate ones over 6–12 months.

↪️
Medium Redirect Hygiene Issue #10

/services route 302-redirects instead of 301

The /services URL returns a 302 (temporary) redirect rather than a 301 (permanent). Search engines treat 302s differently — they may continue crawling the original URL and don't fully pass link equity to the destination.

✓ The fix

Change the redirect type to 301 in the platform configuration. Audit other internal redirects to confirm they're all 301.

📊
Medium Authority Gap Issue #11

Low Authority Score (7) vs Joliet Family Dental (10) — 45× traffic gap

Haven Family Dental holds Authority Score 7 with 98 referring domains (58 of which are spam-grade). Joliet Family Dental holds AS 10 with 174 referring domains and drives 1,259 monthly visits vs Haven's 28. The link asset gap (and contamination) explains most of the traffic gap.

✓ The fix

Begin a Joliet-area citation and backlink program in parallel with the spam disavow — Joliet Chamber of Commerce, Will County dental directories, local-press health columns, Creighton Dental School alumni features.

🔗
Medium Brand & Internal Linking Issue #12

Brand-name dilution + weak internal-link density

The homepage drives 64% of organic traffic but doesn't link prominently to service pages. The brand "Haven" is shared with multiple national dental practices (Haven Dental Studio Chicago, Haven Dentistry Westheimer) — Google can't always disambiguate, causing brand-search authority leakage.

✓ The fix

Add a service-grid block on the homepage with descriptive anchor text. Strengthen the local entity association — consistent "Haven Family Dental of Joliet" anchor text in citations and schema.

Strategic Positioning

The brand entity isn't yet local-anchored

"Haven" is shared with multiple national dental brands. Search-engine confusion is observable — the site ranks for "haven dental studio chicago," "haven dentistry westheimer," and "haven dental studio." Google can't always distinguish which Haven a searcher means.

Recommended brand clarification

From "Haven Family Dental" to "Haven Family Dental of Joliet"

The geographic qualifier in the brand name disambiguates from out-of-state Haven practices and produces a stronger local entity signal across maps, citations, and AI search surfaces. The shift doesn't require legally renaming the practice — it's about consistent anchor text across schema, citations, social profiles, and partnership mentions.
