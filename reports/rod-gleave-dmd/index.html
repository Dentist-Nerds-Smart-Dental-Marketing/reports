What we'd do in Step 1

The site and the reviews are already strong — the gap is visibility. Step 1 is a focused push on local ranking and content (not a rebuild): get the practice into the Murray map pack and give Google and AI real content to rank.

  • Own the Google Business Profile for Murray. Categories, services, geo-tagged photos, weekly posts and Q&A — tracked with Local Falcon until the practice is in the map pack.
  • Disavow the toxic links. ~65% of referring domains sit at authority ≤2 — clean them up, then start quality, local link building.
  • Add FAQs + FAQ schema to every treatment page — patient-language answers structured for both SEO and AI search.
  • Re-target content around "Murray" and the surrounding cities, not just "Salt Lake City," so the practice ranks where it actually is.
  • Expand thin service pages & publish local content to close the 350–660-keyword gap with nearby competitors.
  • Rewrite meta titles & descriptions and tighten internal linking between service pages.

Why this matters: the practice already has the reputation — 4.8★ across 569 reviews — it's just invisible where patients search. Fix local visibility and content, and every other channel compounds on top of it.
