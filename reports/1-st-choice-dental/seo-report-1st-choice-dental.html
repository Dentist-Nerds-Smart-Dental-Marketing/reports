Recommendations
Prioritized Action Plan

What to do next — ordered by impact and effort

🔴 High Priority

Launch a Monthly Blog

The single highest-ROI action available. Publishing 1 article/month targeting dental questions and local keywords will grow traffic significantly within 6–12 months.

  • Start with "dental implant cost in Los Angeles"
  • 500–800 words, include your location naturally
  • Add 1–2 internal links per article to service pages
  • Publish consistently — monthly beats sporadic
🔴 High Priority

Push "first choice dental" into Top 10

This keyword gets 5,400 searches/month and you're at position 17 — tantalizingly close to page 1. A targeted on-page push could deliver a major traffic spike.

  • Add phrase naturally to homepage H1 and meta title
  • Get 2–3 local citations mentioning the exact phrase
  • Build homepage content to 600+ words
🟡 Medium Priority

Improve Authority Score (16 → 25+)

Your authority score of 16 is holding back rankings in competitive searches. Building quality links will lift all rankings.

  • Submit to Zocdoc, Healthgrades, 1-800-Dentist
  • Request mention in a local North Hollywood blog
  • Blog content earns links naturally over time
🟡 Medium Priority

Expand Service Pages with Content

Existing service pages are ranking but thin on content. Fleshing them out to 600+ words with FAQs will improve both rankings and conversions.

  • Add patient FAQ sections to each service page
  • Include local city names naturally
  • Add before/after images with descriptive alt text
🔵 Lower Priority

Target Broader LA Keywords

You're ranking #9 for "best dentist los angeles" — a high-volume term. With stronger content and authority, this becomes a significant traffic driver.

  • Maintain homepage optimization
  • Build reviews on Google & Yelp
  • Blog content will boost this naturally
🔵 Lower Priority

Multilingual Content Opportunity

Your practice serves patients in Spanish and Farsi. Spanish-language blog posts could unlock an untapped keyword market in the LA area.

  • Translate top-performing English pages into Spanish
  • Add hreflang tags for proper Google indexing
  • Target "dentista en north hollywood"
