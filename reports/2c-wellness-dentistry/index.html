ISSUE 1 Critical · Organic Visibility

Zero organic traffic and zero keyword rankings — the site is invisible to new patients searching online.

A brand new website starts with no history in Google’s eyes. The site hasn’t yet earned the trust signals Google needs to begin ranking it for competitive dental queries. At zero keywords ranked, every potential new patient searching “dentist near me,” “teeth cleaning,” or any treatment name is finding competitors instead of 2C Wellness Dentistry.

This is completely normal for a new site — but it doesn’t fix itself on its own. Google needs to see consistent, authoritative content, proper on-page optimization, and incoming links from trusted sources before it starts surfacing the site in search results. The longer this window goes unaddressed, the more ground competitors gain.

✓ The Fix

Launch a structured SEO campaign immediately: optimize every existing page with proper title tags, meta descriptions, header structure, and local signals (city name, service area). Add substantive written content to every treatment page — 500–1,000 words minimum — so Google has enough content to understand, trust, and rank each page. Rankings typically begin emerging within 60–120 days of a sustained content and authority campaign.

ISSUE 2 Critical · Domain Authority

No backlink profile and zero domain authority — Google has no external signals to trust this site yet.

Domain Authority (DA) is a measure of how much Google trusts a website, built primarily through links from other reputable websites pointing to yours. A DA of 0 means the site is brand new and has no external credibility signals. Competitors with DA scores of 20–40+ have a significant ranking head start simply because they’ve been cited, listed, and referenced across the web for years.

Without backlinks, even perfectly written content struggles to rank for competitive keywords. A DA of 0 also directly limits AI visibility — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google’s AI Overview all weight domain authority when deciding which practices to surface in AI-generated answers.

✓ The Fix

Begin building a starter backlink profile immediately: get the practice listed on all major dental and local business directories (Yelp, Healthgrades, ZocDoc, WebMD, RateMDs, Bing Places), submit to dental association directories, and pursue local citation building. Dentist Nerds will build and manage this initial backlink foundation to steadily raise DA and improve both Google rankings and AI search visibility.

ISSUE 3 Critical · Content Gap

No dedicated pages for preventative, cosmetic, and key restorative dentistry — impossible to rank for those treatments without them.

While the site has content and dedicated pages for some treatments, entire service categories are currently missing dedicated pages — specifically preventative dentistry, cosmetic dentistry (teeth whitening, veneers, bonding, smile makeovers), and several restorative dentistry services. Google ranks pages, not websites. If there’s no page for “teeth whitening,” the site simply cannot appear when a patient searches that term.

Each missing treatment category represents a cluster of high-intent local search queries that 2C Wellness Dentistry is currently invisible for. Patients searching “cosmetic dentist near me,” “dental cleaning [city],” or “teeth whitening [city]” will never find this practice until those pages exist.

✓ The Fix

Build dedicated, content-rich pages for every missing treatment category: Preventative Care (cleanings, exams, X-rays, fluoride, sealants), Cosmetic Dentistry (veneers, bonding, whitening, smile makeovers), and all remaining restorative services. Each page needs 500–1,000+ words with FAQ sections, local signals, and proper internal linking. Dentist Nerds will research, write, and deliver all missing pages as part of the onboarding campaign.

ISSUE 4 High · Blog & Content Authority

Only 2 blogs on the site — both don’t meet SEO standards, and blog content is one of the fastest ways to grow keyword rankings.

The site currently has two blog posts that don’t meet the content depth, word count, or structural requirements needed to rank or drive organic traffic. For a new site with zero domain authority, a consistent blogging strategy is one of the fastest levers to pull — each well-written blog post is a new page that can rank for its own keywords and bring in new patients.

Practices that publish 4+ quality blog posts per month consistently outgrow competitors in organic keyword count because they’re creating dozens of new ranking opportunities every year. Over 12 months, 4 blogs/month compounds into 48 new indexed pages, each pulling in their own traffic.

✓ The Fix

Dentist Nerds will publish 4 professionally written, SEO-optimized blog posts per month. Each post will target specific keywords with real local search volume, include proper internal links to relevant treatment pages, and be structured to rank in both traditional Google search and AI-powered tools like Google AI Overview and ChatGPT.

ISSUE 5 High · Google Business Profile

Only 9 Google reviews — far below the threshold patients and Google need to trust and surface a new practice.

Google reviews are one of the most powerful local ranking signals. Practices with 80–200+ reviews consistently outrank those with fewer reviews for “dentist near me” and service-based local queries. At just 9 reviews, 2C Wellness Dentistry is well below the competitive threshold — and patients comparing practices on Google Maps will gravitate toward those with more social proof.

Reviews also directly impact visibility in the local 3-pack — the map results that appear above organic links. Review volume is one of the strongest signals Google uses to decide which practices appear there.
