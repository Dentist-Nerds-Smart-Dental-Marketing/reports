The Systemic Dentist

Comprehensive SEO and AI-visibility audit of systemicdds.com — the Santa Monica biological & holistic dental practice of Dr. Alireza Panahpour, "The Systemic Dentist." The site has built a deep library of educational content on cavitations, mercury removal, root-canal alternatives and biomimetic dentistry, yet it pulls just 23 organic visits a month, ranks for only 71 keywords (down from a peak of 161), and has no dedicated treatment pages, no FAQ content and no schema markup for AI search. This audit shows exactly what is capping growth — and the path to 400+ ranking keywords.

Zero FAQ sections and no FAQ, Service, Dentist or LocalBusiness schema across the site. This is the #1 reason the practice is invisible to AI search — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews rely on structured data and Q&A content to surface and cite a practice.
0
No Dedicated Treatment / Service Pages
Core services — cavitation surgery, mercury removal, biological root-canal alternatives, biomimetic & cosmetic dentistry, ozone therapy, TMJ — have no individual, optimized landing pages. Discovery and high-intent search has nowhere to land, so the practice captures almost no new-patient traffic.
!
Usability & ADA Accessibility Issues
The site has usability friction and ADA (WCAG) accessibility gaps — a compliance and legal risk for a healthcare website, and a drag on both conversions and Google engagement signals. The foundation needs to be rebuilt to be accessible, fast and conversion-friendly.
224

224 backlinks across 127 referring domains, but much of the profile is low-quality and spam-adjacent. It needs a full evaluation, disavow/clean-up of toxic links, and a deliberate monthly high-authority link-building plan so the referring domains finally translate into ranking power.
127
A Distinctive Brand & Content Library to Build On
127 referring domains and a deep, genuinely unique content library — "The Systemic Dentist," cavitations, mercury removal, root-canal alternatives — give the site a real foundation and a defensible niche. Organic Search Optimization

6-Step Process

A proven six-step process that combines expert strategy and advanced tools to dominate organic search across Google, Maps, and local directories in Santa Monica, Los Angeles and the surrounding communities.

STEP 01
Comprehensive Audit
Analyze current strategy, target ZIP codes across Santa Monica and greater Los Angeles, and top biological-dentistry competitors to identify growth opportunities.
STEP 02
Fix the Foundation
Rebuild the website on a strong technical foundation for SEO and AI visibility — resolve usability and ADA accessibility issues, fix the schema markup, and make every page fast and conversion-friendly.
STEP 03
Local Listings & NAP Accuracy
Clean up and optimize listings across all major directories, keeping Name, Address, and Phone consistent, and manage the Google Business Profile & Maps every month.
STEP 04
Treatment Pages + Schema
Build and optimize a dedicated page for every service — cavitation surgery, mercury removal, root-canal alternatives, biomimetic & cosmetic dentistry, TMJ — with proper H1/H2 structure, FAQ content, and Dentist/LocalBusiness/Service schema.
STEP 05
Backlink Clean-Up & Authority
Evaluate the profile, clean up spam and toxic links, then build high-quality follow backlinks from high-authority websites every month so the 127 referring domains finally translate into ranking power.
STEP 06
Monthly Reporting & Tracking
Detailed monthly reports and Semrush position tracking to monitor rankings, traffic and conversions — analyzing and improving the site every single month.
