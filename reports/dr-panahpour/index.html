Executive Summary

Key Findings

Very Low Organic Traffic & Authority — 23 Visits / mo, AS 7
The practice earns roughly 23 organic visits a month on an Authority Score of just 7. Over half of that traffic lands on a single educational page (/cavitation/, 12 visits), with the rest spread thin across a TMJ FAQ post and a technology page. For an established Los Angeles biological-dentistry practice, this is a fraction of the visibility it should hold.
Only 71 Keywords Ranking — and Declining from 161
A practice spanning cavitation surgery, mercury (amalgam) removal, root-canal alternatives, biomimetic and cosmetic dentistry, and TMJ should rank for hundreds of keywords. It ranks for 71 — down from a peak of 161 in April 2025. The keyword footprint is shrinking year-over-year, and almost every term sits on page 3–10 with no real traffic. Position tracking in Semrush should be set up to monitor this monthly.
No Treatment Pages & No FAQ Content — Hurting AI Visibility
The site is built almost entirely from blog and condition articles. There are no dedicated, conversion-focused treatment/service pages, and no FAQ sections or FAQ/Service schema anywhere. This is one of the biggest reasons the practice is invisible to AI search — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews rely on structured Q&A content to understand, trust and cite a dental practice. Without it, these pages stay buried on page 3–10.
Usability & ADA Accessibility Issues
The site carries usability problems and ADA (accessibility) compliance gaps that both frustrate visitors and create legal exposure — a real risk for healthcare websites. Poor usability also suppresses conversions and sends weak engagement signals to Google, compounding the ranking problem. The foundation needs to be rebuilt to be accessible, fast and conversion-friendly.
Backlink Profile Needs a Spam Clean-Up
There are 224 backlinks across 127 referring domains, but much of the profile is low-quality and spam-adjacent — links that pass little authority and can actively hold the domain back. The profile needs a full evaluation and clean-up of toxic/spam links, paired with a deliberate, monthly high-authority link-building plan so the practice finally accrues the authority it needs to rank.
