Executive summary

Other Key Findings · Adel Dentistry

◈
Veneers is the strongest commercial asset — and it's being wasted

The practice has a dedicated /porcelain-veneers-tampa-fl page targeting a high-value cosmetic keyword (porcelain veneers tampa, 90 searches/mo). It currently ranks at #77 — deep on page 8. A properly structured, schema-marked veneers page for a Carrollwood Tampa practice could realistically reach top 20 within one quarter, driving high-intent traffic worth $4–9 CPC in paid equivalency.

↓
Wix platform is limiting SEO ceiling

URL slugs like /about-1, /categories-1, and /gallery-static are Wix template artifacts — non-descriptive paths that dilute crawl signals and prevent page-level topic authority. Wix sites can rank well, but only with deliberate structural cleanup that most Wix users never do. The current setup signals to Google that the site is a default installation rather than an intentionally built practice presence.

⚡
Traffic collapsed after launch peak — now slowly recovering

The site launched around October 2024 and briefly saw 35–62 visits per month in its first two months before dropping to 3–11 visits per month through most of 2025. May 2026 shows 15 visits — a recovery, but still far below even the modest early peak. Keyword count has grown consistently (now 110) which means Google is indexing more content, but rankings are too deep to convert to clicks.

↑
Competing with a site (Phoenix Dental Tampa) that has far fewer backlinks but double the traffic

Phoenix Dental Tampa has only 37 referring domains vs. Adel Dentistry's 92 — yet drives 82 monthly visits vs. 15. Their Authority Score is 16 vs. 7. This shows that content optimization and on-page structure matter more than raw link count at this local competitive level. Better-structured pages on similar domain authority will outrank a larger backlink profile every time.
