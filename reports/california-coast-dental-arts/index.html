Diagnosis

Key Findings

Cal Coast Dental Arts is not in crisis — it is an improvement opportunity. These six findings mix the strengths worth building on with the gaps holding back conversions and high-value rankings.

!
Stock Images Are Killing Conversions
The site leans heavily on stock photography, which signals inauthenticity. Patients book where they feel trust — real photos of the doctor, team, and office convert 2–3× better than stock imagery.
~
Service Pages Are Too Thin
Treatment pages lack the depth Google needs to rank them for commercial queries. Competitors with richer, more complete service content win those high-value searches.
✓
Video Content Is a Real Advantage
The site already uses video effectively — this is rare among local practices and valuable for engagement and dwell time. Dentist Nerds will build on this foundation rather than starting from zero.
◆
Schema Markup Already Deployed
Structured data is in place. With deeper content on service pages, that schema will amplify rankings and increase eligibility for Google AI Overview citations.
✓
Monthly Blogs Driving Long-Tail Traffic
The blog is actively producing content and already ranks for technology and treatment queries like “modern dental technology” and “new dental technology.” A healthy cadence is in place.
~
Commercial-Intent Visibility Gap
Just 6.4% of commercial search share despite 528 ranked keywords. Implants and cosmetic queries need dedicated, deep service pages to capture the patients actively ready to book.

The Plan

Growth Opportunities

Six moves that convert Cal Coast’s existing authority into booked appointments and durable rankings.

01
Real Photos
Replace all stock imagery with authentic photos of the practice, team, and Dr. Trust signals translate directly into more booked appointments.
02
Service Page Content Upgrade
Implants, cosmetic, Invisalign, emergency, periodontics — deeper copy, FAQs, before/after galleries, and patient stories to win commercial queries.
03
GBP & Local Pack
“Dentist Oceanside” pulls ~390/mo. A fully built Google Business Profile plus a review engine earns a Local Pack entry.
04
Blog → AI Visibility
4 posts/month structured for Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT citations, with FAQ schema on every post.
05
Ongoing Link Building
Grow Authority Score from 10 → 18–22. Target North County San Diego directories, the Chamber, and dental associations.
06
Video + Schema Amplification
Already has a video advantage. Add VideoObject schema and embed videos on service pages for a measurable dwell-time boost.
