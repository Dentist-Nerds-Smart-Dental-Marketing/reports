What we'd do in Step 1

The good news: you don't need a rebuild. With 1,373 ranking keywords already in place, the content footprint is there — it's just sitting on page 2+. Step 1 is about turning those near-misses into page-1 rankings and fixing the issues that hold them back.

  • Fix ADA accessibility first. The site came back non-compliant — close the contrast, alt-text and labeled-form gaps. It's a patient-access and legal priority.
  • Push page-2 pages onto page 1. Strengthen the all-on-4, crowns, restoration & Sherman Oaks implant pages with better content, internal links and on-page SEO.
  • Add FAQs to every treatment page — in patient language, with FAQPage schema for both SEO and AI search.
  • Schema markup across the site — Dentist, MedicalProcedure, FAQPage, AggregateRating.
  • Swap stock photos for real ones — authentic doctor, team & office imagery builds trust and conversions.
  • Tighten UX & resolve duplicate URLs — clear navigation, obvious book/call CTAs, one canonical page per keyword.

Why this matters: the hard part — building a content footprint — is already done. Optimizing what exists is faster and cheaper than a rebuild, and it's what lets authority & reviews compound the rankings up from page 2.
