Executive Summary

Key Findings

Catastrophic 4-Year Traffic Decline
Peak of 692 visits in May 2022 has fallen to 31 in March 2026 — a 95.5% decline. The site has been losing organic visibility steadily since mid-2022 with no recovery trend.
Critically Thin Site Architecture
Only 6 indexed URLs exist — homepage, about Dr. Wong, contact, dental insurance, and one services overview page. There are no individual treatment pages, no blog content, and no location-specific landing pages.
Mostly Branded Traffic — No Discovery Engine
Of the 31 monthly visits, the vast majority are people searching "dr william wong" or "doctor william wong" by name. Generic dental-intent keywords like "dentist santa monica" sit at positions 26+ and drive zero traffic.
Strong Backlink Profile Going Unused
142 referring domains and 226 backlinks — surprisingly strong for a site this small. The off-page authority is there, but with so few pages and no fresh content, there's nothing for that authority to lift in search.
Mixed HTTP/HTTPS Indexing
The top-traffic URL is on http:// (12 visits), while the secondary URL is on https:// (7 visits). Two protocols ranking for the same pages splits link equity and confuses Google about the canonical version.

Implementation Approach · Process 01

Organic Search Optimization

6-Step Process

A proven six-step process that combines expert strategy and advanced tools to dominate organic search results across Google, Maps, and local directories.

STEP 01
Comprehensive Audit
Analyze current strategy, target ZIP codes, and top competitors to identify growth opportunities.
STEP 02
Technical & On-Page Setup
Build or optimize the website to ensure every technical element and on-page detail is set up correctly.
STEP 03
Local Listings & NAP Accuracy
Secure and optimize profiles across all major directories, keeping Name, Address, and Phone consistent.
STEP 04
Custom Content Creation
Dental writers craft unique blogs and treatment pages designed to rank on Google and AI-driven platforms.
STEP 05
Authority-Building Backlinks
Through a trusted network, earn high-quality backlinks and brand exposure to boost domain authority.
STEP 06
Ongoing Reporting & Tracking
Detailed monthly reports and review meetings to track rankings, conversions, and measurable progress.
