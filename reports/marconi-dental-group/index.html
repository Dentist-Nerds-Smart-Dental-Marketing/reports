Executive Summary · Your Questions, Answered

Key Findings

#1 Weakness — high traffic, but the wrong audience
The single biggest issue: ~80% of the site's 2,309 monthly visits come from two viral blog posts about dental anesthesia ("how long does novocaine/lidocaine last"). These are national informational searches — readers anywhere, not Carmichael patients. The numbers look great, but almost none of this traffic can become a patient. Redirecting that hard-won authority toward local, patient-intent searches is the highest-leverage move available.
The money pages are invisible
The high-value local terms — "dental implants sacramento" (720/mo, $14.75 CPC) and "cosmetic dentist sacramento" (590/mo) — barely rank, and the implant, All-on-4 and cosmetic pages each draw about four visits a month. For a practice whose Dr. Visagé specializes in implants, that's the single biggest revenue gap on the site.
79% of traffic rests on two posts — and they're sliding
Concentrating four-fifths of all visits on two anesthesia articles is a real risk: they've already fallen ~40% from the February 2026 peak (3,819 → 2,309). A single algorithm update could wipe out most of the site's traffic. Durable growth needs a broader base of local pages.
Real authority — just pointed the wrong way
Authority Score 11, 223 referring domains, and 1,553 ranking keywords is a genuine SEO engine most practices never build. The problem isn't capability — it's direction. That same authority, aimed at "dental implants sacramento" and the treatment pages, would convert into actual patients.
What's working — content SEO and a 4.8★ reputation
Ranking #1–#3 nationally for high-volume terms proves the content engine genuinely works — most dental sites can't do this. Pair that with an outstanding 4.8★ rating across 760+ reviews (detailed in the GBP scorecard below), and the raw materials for local dominance are all here; they just need re-aiming.

Triage Center · Question 4

Technical & AI Issues

AI
AI Visibility — engines know the blog, not the dentist
Because the strong content is about anesthesia, AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) are far more likely to cite Marconi for "how long does novocaine last" than to recommend it as a Carmichael implant or cosmetic dentist. Adding Dentist / LocalBusiness / Service / FAQ schema and local, patient-intent content would make AI surface the practice for the searches that actually bring patients.
404
"Trashed" pages still indexed
Deleted URLs containing "__trashed" (e.g. /fair-oaks-ca__trashed/wisdom-teeth-extractions/, /fair-oaks-ca__trashed/pediatric-dentist/) are still indexed and ranking. These leftover, orphaned pages waste crawl budget, confuse Google about the live URLs, and should be properly redirected or removed.
