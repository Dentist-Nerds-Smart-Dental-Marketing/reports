How Dentist Nerds Fixes This

We don't believe in flashy launches that flame out. The plan starts with a strong foundation, then layers ongoing engines on top — each one compounding the last.

The Foundation
Optimize the foundation you already have.

What we'd do in Step 1

Marconi already has a deep content library — over 1,500 ranking keywords. This isn't a rebuild; it's an unlock. We fix what's keeping those pages off page 1 and close the compliance gaps, so the ongoing engines below have a clean base to compound on.

  • Fix ADA accessibility. The site came back non-compliant — close the contrast, alt-text and labeled-form gaps our review flagged.
  • Optimize the local money pages. Implants, hygienist and core service pages exist but rank page 5+ — rework on-page content, titles and internal links so they can climb.
  • Add FAQPage schema to treatment pages. The FAQs are already written — they just need structured markup for rich results and AI answers.
  • Realign content toward local commercial terms. The footprint skews informational; point that authority at the searches that book patients.
  • Replace stock imagery with real photos of the doctors, team & office to build trust and authenticity.
  • Tighten usability — clearer navigation and obvious book/call CTAs on every key page.

Why this matters: the content is already there — fixing the foundation is what lets every other channel (reviews, backlinks, content, AI search) compound instead of leak.
