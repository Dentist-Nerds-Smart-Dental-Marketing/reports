Executive Summary

Key Findings

Organic Momentum Has Slipped Since February
Ranking keywords peaked at 1,113 in February 2026 and have since fallen to 846, while monthly organic traffic has pulled back from ~728 to 465. The site built real momentum through 2024–25 (peaking at 926 visits in August 2025) but is now trending down — a signal that the content and technical foundation need attention before the decline compounds.
Conversion Path Leaks — No Hero CTA, No Homepage Booking Widget
The hero has no call-to-action button, and there's no embedded online-booking/appointment widget on the homepage — so an interested patient has nothing obvious to click at the two highest-intent moments. For a practice earning hundreds of monthly visits, this quietly costs bookings every single day.
Duplicate & Disorganized Services List
The services are listed in a duplicated, disorganized way — overlapping categories and repeated entries that confuse patients and dilute the signals search engines and AI use to understand what the practice offers. A clean, consolidated, well-structured service architecture is needed so each treatment has one clear, authoritative page.
No FAQ Content or Schema on Treatment Pages — Hurting AI Visibility
The treatment pages have no FAQ sections and no FAQPage schema. They carry basic Dentist and review (AggregateRating) schema, but without Q&A content and FAQ markup, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews have little structured information to surface and cite — leaving the practice underrepresented in AI search.
Heavy Branded Dependency & Thin Treatment-Page Traffic
The homepage alone carries 57% of all organic traffic, and roughly 45% of visits come from branded searches like "williamsburg dental arts." Treatment pages rank well for local cosmetic terms but each pulls only a handful of visits. The practice is largely found by people who already know it — not discovered by new patients searching for treatments.
Backlink Profile Needs Evaluation
159 referring domains, 291 total backlinks and Authority Score 13 — a modest base for a Brooklyn practice in a competitive market. The profile has never been deliberately built. A full evaluation plus targeted high-authority link-building is needed to lift the treatment pages and reverse the recent decline.
