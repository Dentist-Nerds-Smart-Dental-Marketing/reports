Executive Summary · Your Questions, Answered

Key Findings

The asset — 292 reviews at a flawless 5.0★
This is the strongest reputation in the audit and it is not close. 292 patients, not one of them below five stars. Reviews are among the heaviest signals Google's Map Pack weighs, and one of the main things AI assistants read when a patient asks for a dentist recommendation. Every other finding in this report is about the fact that this asset is doing far less work than it could.
#1 Weakness — there is no website, and the "Website" button proves it
The Google Business Profile's website field points to linkedin.com/in/paul-ford-…. That's the single most valuable click on the listing — the patient who has read the reviews and is deciding — and it currently lands on a personal LinkedIn page with no services, no hours in context, no booking, and no reason to choose this practice over the next one. Everything downstream of that click is lost.
Four El Cerrito practices split 793 organic visits a month
Dentists of El Cerrito Plaza pulls 528 visits/mo off 302 ranking keywords and owns position #1 for "el cerrito dentist" (210/mo, $5.57 CPC), "dentist el cerrito," "dentist el cerrito ca" and "cerrito dental" ($12.12 CPC). El Cerrito Dentistry adds 140, El Cerrito Dental Care 107, Dr. Bahrami 18. Ford Dental's share of that traffic is zero — and it is zero by construction, not by ranking.
The obvious domain is already parked by someone else
We checked. forddental.com resolves to a domain-parking lander (Semrush: 1 keyword, 0 traffic) — registered, not in use, not the practice. fordentalcare.com is listed for sale on HugeDomains. Domain selection needs a decision early, and the good local variants — a name paired with "El Cerrito" — are still open. This is a small thing that gets more expensive the longer it waits.
Invisible to AI assistants, for a structural reason
ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews build local recommendations from sources they can read and cite. A Google listing contributes; an owned site with service pages, FAQs and schema contributes far more, and is what lets an assistant say why to pick a practice. With zero owned pages there is nothing to cite, so the 292-review advantage rarely reaches the patient who asks an AI instead of searching.
One Google category, and no services listed
The profile carries a single category — "Dentist" — with no secondary categories and no service list attached. Competitors in this market run multiple categories (cosmetic dentist, dental implants periodontist, teeth whitening service) and populate their service menus, which is how they surface for treatment-specific searches rather than only for "dentist." This is free to fix and does not depend on the website.

Triage Center

Presence & Technical Issues

01
The "Website" button sends patients to LinkedIn
The highest-intent click on the Google listing — from a patient who has read the reviews and is choosing — resolves to linkedin.com/in/paul-ford-…. Until a site exists, this field should point somewhere that can actually convert; once it does, this is the first thing to update. Fixable in minutes, and worth doing before anything else.
02
No owned domain — and the obvious one is taken
Verified: forddental.com serves a parking lander and shows 1 keyword / 0 traffic in Semrush — registered by someone else, not the practice. fordentalcare.com is for sale on HugeDomains. A locally-qualified variant should be secured now; it is the cheapest decision in this report and the only one with a deadline attached.
03
Single Google category, no service menu
The profile runs one category — "Dentist" — with no secondaries and no services attached. Adding accurate secondary categories and populating the service list is free, takes an afternoon, and is the only lever in this report that can lift Map Pack visibility for treatment searches without a website existing yet.
04
No structured data anywhere — nothing for AI to read
Schema markup (Dentist, Service, FAQPage, AggregateRating) is how a practice tells search engines and AI assistants what it is, what it treats, and how patients rate it. With no site, none of it exists. This is why the 292 reviews, which should be decisive, rarely surface in an AI answer about El Cerrito dentists.
05
No owned booking path
Every appointment currently starts with a phone call or a third-party listing. Without an owned page there is no online booking, no new-patient form, no way to capture the patient who is comparing practices at 9pm — and no measurement of how many were lost.
