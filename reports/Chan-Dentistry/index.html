Where Are the Areas of Opportunity?

Six concrete opportunities the new owner can capitalize on. Each is sized using Semrush search-volume data, Dr. Chan's existing clinical credentials, and Huntington Beach market estimates. Several are uncontested — meaning a new owner can own them without displacing any incumbent.

Opportunity 01

Pinhole Surgical Technique — Uncontested

Dr. Chan is a certified Pinhole Academy member trained in Pinhole Surgical Technique for gum recession — a high-margin, niche service. No Huntington Beach competitor offers it or ranks for it. "Pinhole gum surgery", "pinhole technique near me", "gum recession treatment Orange County" are wide open. The new owner inherits a uncontested specialty differentiator.

Est. uplift: 30–60 cases/yr · $90K–$300K revenue

Opportunity 02

Fix HTTP/HTTPS Canonicalization

The site's traffic is split between http:// and https:// versions of the homepage — Semrush shows 45 sessions on HTTP and 26 on HTTPS as separate pages. Resolving this and consolidating link equity could deliver a 15–30% rankings lift in 30 days with no content investment. Easiest win available to the new owner.

Est. uplift: +15–25 visits/mo immediately

Opportunity 03

"Huntington Beach Dentist" Top-3 Push

Currently ranks #66 for "huntington beach dentists" (170/mo, $6.92 CPC), #52 for "best dentist in huntington beach ca" (90/mo, $4.08 CPC), #67 for "dentist in huntington beach" (260/mo). Combined ~520 monthly searches at high CPC. With proper service pages and link building, top-10 is achievable in 6–9 months for the new owner.

Est. uplift: 80–160 new patients/yr

Opportunity 04

Activate Implant + Cosmetic Credentials

Dr. Chan completed his fellowship in Implant Surgery (Implant Seminars + California Implant Institute) and an Aesthetic Dentistry residency at Esthetic Professionals — top-tier credentials. The implant page has 1 keyword, 0 traffic. A proper implant + cosmetic content strategy is the highest-value rebuild for the new owner ($3K–$30K per implant case).

Est. uplift: $250K–$600K/yr revenue

Opportunity 05

Bella Terra / Hamilton Geo Pages

The practice sits at 9917 Hamilton Avenue near Brookhurst — a high-traffic intersection adjacent to Bella Terra mall. No competitor targets these neighborhood searches. Geo-targeted pages for "dentist near Bella Terra", "dentist near Hamilton/Brookhurst", "dentist near Trader Joe's Huntington Beach" are wide open at near-zero competition.

Est. uplift: 30–60 new patients/yr

Opportunity 06

Invisalign & Zoom Whitening Activation

Invisalign page has 2 ranking keywords, 0 traffic. Zoom Whitening — a brand-name service that converts strongly — has no dedicated page at all. Both services already exist clinically; both are missing from search. Quick rebuilds with high commercial intent for the new owner.

Est. uplift: 20–40 cases/yr · $80K–$200K

Combined Year-1 opportunity sizing for the new owner: $420K–$1.1M revenue uplift, primarily from implant and Pinhole Surgical Technique cases. Question 4 of 4
04
What Can We Do to Help?

Nine specific deliverables structured into three 30-day phases for the new owner. By Day 90 the digital foundation will be rebuilt, the on-page and technical issues will be resolved, and the content engine will be running for ongoing share-of-voice growth.

Phase 1 · Days 1–30 — Foundation

01

Fix HTTP/HTTPS & Technical Foundation

Consolidate to single HTTPS canonical, set 301 redirects from HTTP versions, fix Search Console verification, audit robots.txt, ensure proper indexation. The fastest-impact lever available to the new owner.

02

Deploy Schema Site-Wide

Dentist + LocalBusiness schema. Person schema for the practicing dentist with credentials (Pinhole Academy, implant fellowships, JHU magna cum laude, UCSF DDS, cosmetic residency). FAQ schema on every service page.

03

Claim Missing Directories

Healthgrades, Zocdoc, ADA Find-A-Dentist, Bing Places, Apple Maps, NextDoor, RealSelf, 1-800-Dentist, Pinhole Academy member directory. Photos, services, hours, NAP-consistent across all citations.

Phase 2 · Days 31–60 — Content & Visibility

04

Build 12 Service Pages from Scratch

Pinhole Surgical Technique (priority), dental implants, Invisalign, Zoom whitening, porcelain veneers, cosmetic dentistry, laser dentistry, oral sedation, crowns, fillings, cleanings, emergency dentist. 1,500–2,500 words each, FAQ schema, before/after photos, cost ranges.

05

Launch Review Engine

Birdeye/Podium-class automation with post-visit SMS + email. The practice has strong reviews (100% recommend on Facebook, 28+ reviews) but they're not aggregating on Google. Target 15–25 new Google reviews/month for the new owner.

06

Pinhole Surgical Technique Vertical

Dedicated content cluster: main service page + 4–6 supporting articles ("Pinhole vs gum graft", "Pinhole recovery time", "Pinhole cost", "Is Pinhole right for me"). Pinhole Academy backlink. AI-Overview targeting. Owns an uncontested vertical.

Phase 3 · Days 61–90 — Expansion & Authority

07

Local Backlink Campaign

Huntington Beach Chamber of Commerce, OC Register, OC Weekly, Pinhole Academy directory, dental association directories, USCF alumni network, local sponsorships. Target: 25+ quality DA 30+ links Year 1 for the new owner.

08

Neighborhood Geo Pages

Bella Terra dentist, Hamilton/Brookhurst dentist, Adams Avenue dentist, Pacific Coast Highway dentist, Fountain Valley-adjacent dentist, Newport Beach-adjacent dentist. All currently uncontested or low-competition.

09

Content Engine + AEO

2 long-form blog posts/month targeting Oceanic and Mortensen's keyword wins. FAQ-pattern content for AI Overview citation. Expand smile gallery and patient stories. Each post links to a relevant treatment page.

Projected 12-month outcome for the new owner: Commercial-intent search share grows from ~0.6% → ~12–18%, putting the practice in striking distance of Splendid Dentistry and meaningfully closing the gap with Mortensen and Sea Cliff. Organic traffic grows from 71/mo → 350–500/mo with most growth coming from commercial searches, not name-based queries. Total Year-1 revenue uplift: $420K–$1.1M. This is the rebuild a buyer should price into the acquisition model — the practice has the clinical capability already; what it lacks is the digital scaffolding to convert that capability into new-patient flow.

🚨HTTP/HTTPS Canonicalization (Critical)

Both http://www.chandentistry.com/ AND https://www.chandentistry.com/ are indexed as separate pages. 63% of organic traffic still routes through the insecure HTTP version. This is splitting link equity, hurting trust signals, and creating duplicate content issues. Must be resolved before any other SEO work.

⚠️No Content Strategy

Zero blog posts. No service-page content beyond surface-level descriptions. No FAQ content. No before/after gallery. The site reads as a 2010-era brochure, not a 2026 patient acquisition asset.

⚠️Mobile & Speed

Full mobile/Core Web Vitals audit recommended. Long-running site likely accumulating bloat. Mobile experience particularly important for a dental practice — most "dentist near me" searches happen on mobile.

🔗Internal Linking & Information Architecture

With only 8 pages, there is barely an internal link structure to evaluate. Service pages don't cross-link. The homepage doesn't funnel authority to specific treatment pages. The "Meet Dr. Chan" page — which holds all the credential authority — doesn't link to or strengthen any service page. A proper site rebuild for the new owner would treat Dr. Chan's credentials as a topical authority hub linking to every relevant service.
