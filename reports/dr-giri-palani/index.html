Executive summary

Other Key Findings · What Needs Improvement

⚠
National blog content is carrying drpalani.com instead of commercial pages

Top traffic pages are blog posts about coconut oil for tooth pain (480/mo), super glue on teeth (260/mo), best gum for bad breath (1,300/mo), bruising after wisdom teeth, and fluoride allergy — all national informational queries. Local implant and cosmetic service pages drive ~6 visits/mo combined. Wrong audience for the practice's revenue model.

⚠
Beverly Hills satellites running on a legacy ASP template

All four Beverly Hills domains use an identical /p/Dental-Implants-Beverly-Hills-… ASP URL structure and templated service pages — same legacy CMS, same era. The shared template generates many 301 redirects and 404 pages today, fragmenting authority across multiple dormant properties.

⚠
Growth on drpalani.com has not reached commercial pages

Total traffic nearly doubled over 12 months (456 → 903 visits), but every visit gained came through the blog layer. The implant, cosmetic, sedation, and full-mouth service pages — the pages that produce consultations — have not participated in the growth.

⚠
Reputation signal not surfacing in search results across the portfolio

120 reviews at 4.8★ plus Dr. Palani's FICOI implant-specialty credential are real assets — but no LocalBusiness, AggregateRating, MedicalProcedure, or FAQPage schema exists on any of the five domains. None of this reputation appears in rich-result panels or AI Overviews.

Property 05 · Offline

ranchopalosverdesdentist.com

Status

The only domain matching the practice's city is offline

DNS does not resolve

A direct request to https://ranchopalosverdesdentist.com/ returns "The remote name could not be resolved." The domain is either expired, never delegated, or pointed at no host. The domain has also fallen out of every major organic search index, suggesting it has been offline for an extended period.

What was lost: the domain name itself is the most valuable real estate Dr. Palani could own — an exact match for the practice's city. It would have been a natural anchor for local SEO, "rancho palos verdes dentist" anchor-text backlinks, and a primary entity for Google to associate with the practice. Today, 26 referring domains still link to URLs on this domain, and all of those links return errors.

What to do about it

Options for the domain

1
Re-register and revive the domain (preferred)

If the registration has lapsed, re-acquire it. Build it back as either a primary RPV-targeted site or 301-redirect everything to drpalani.com — either way recovers the 26 referring domains' worth of link equity that's currently orphaned.

2
Verify domain ownership status

Check WHOIS to confirm whether Dr. Palani still owns the registration (just inactive DNS) or whether it has lapsed and is available. If still owned, restoring DNS is a small technical fix. If lapsed, registration is the first step.

3
If unrecoverable: redirect all referring domains

If the domain has been claimed by someone else or cannot be re-acquired, reach out to the 26 referring sites and request that links be updated to point at drpalani.com. This is slow manual work but preserves at least some of the link equity.
