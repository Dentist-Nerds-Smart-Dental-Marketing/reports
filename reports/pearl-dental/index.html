Issue 01 — AI search visibility

17,145 monthly visits — but invisible to AI Overviews, ChatGPT & Perplexity

0
pages with schema markup

No LocalBusiness, MedicalProcedure, FAQPage, AggregateRating, or Article schema is deployed anywhere on the site. AI search engines — Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot — read schema to pick up procedure data, provider credentials, ratings, and FAQ answers. Pearl Dental Group's content engine works for traditional search but isn't yet eligible for the AI-search layer that increasingly drives clicks. The 540+ reviews, the "Family Favorites" awards, and the cost-guide answers are all invisible to those channels.

The legacy batonrouge.pearldentalgroup.com subdomain returns "Just a moment..." and "Please Wait..." Cloudflare-challenge pages on every crawl attempt — 30+ URLs indexed but all blocked. This subdomain still receives backlinks but cannot pass any equity to the main site.

~30 /blog/ URL 301 redirects from prior content migration

The migration that moved cost-guide content out of /blog/ into top-level URLs left ~30 legacy redirects. One in particular — /blog/dental-implants-near-me/ — still receives 46 referring-domain backlinks routed through a 301. Worth auditing the chain length and consolidation opportunities.

Executive summary

Other Key Findings · What Needs Improvement

⚠
Cost-guide visitors aren't converting into local-patient leads

Pages like /full-mouth-dental-implants-cost/ (2,414 visits) attract national informational searchers — patients researching procedures, not Baton Rouge prospects ready to book. Without strong local-pricing context, in-page CTAs to the three offices, and internal-link bridges to matching service pages, this traffic is unconverted.

⚠
Commercial service pages drive less than 1.5% of total traffic

Despite a 17,145-visit month, the dedicated service pages (/dental-implants/ 115 visits, /cosmetic-dentistry/ 62, /services/emergency-dentistry/ 21, /dental-implants/all-on-4-dental-implants/ 34) together account for under 250 monthly visits. The content engine works; the commercial bottleneck does not.

⚠
Metairie location pages need more SEO depth

The Veterans Blvd / Metairie location pages — /locations-metairie/veterans-blvd/ (90 visits) and the Metairie service pages — have meaningful potential but currently produce a fraction of the Baton Rouge Perkins/Jones Creek locations' traffic. Metairie is a separate metro, and the practice's footprint there is thinner than its Baton Rouge presence.

⚠
Reputation signal not surfacing in schema

540+ reviews at 5.0★ across multiple platforms (Birdeye, Yelp, Google) plus four consecutive "Family Favorites Dentists" wins from Baton Rouge Parents magazine. Strong asset, but no LocalBusiness, AggregateRating, or Award schema on the homepage to broadcast it into rich results and AI Overviews.

⚠
One 404 still indexed

/schedule?ao=Profiles returns Page Not Found but is still indexed. Minor compared to the subdomain issue but worth removing from the sitemap and submitting for de-indexing.

Where Pearl Dental Group Needs Help

⚠
AI search readiness — schema across the site

Zero schema markup deployed despite the volume. Needs help building out LocalBusiness, MedicalProcedure, FAQPage, AggregateRating, and Article schema so the existing reputation and content surfaces in AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity — the channels increasingly drawing clicks away from blue links.

⚠
Bridging content traffic into commercial leads

The cost-guide and educational pages pull 16,000+ monthly visits, but commercial service pages capture under 250. Needs help adding in-page CTAs, location-specific phone routing, internal-link bridges, and Baton Rouge/Metairie pricing context that turn an informational reader into a consultation request.

⚠
Metairie market footprint expansion

The Veterans Blvd Metairie location performs at a fraction of the Baton Rouge offices. Needs help building Metairie-specific service pages, locally-targeted FAQ content, schema for the New Orleans-area entity, and citation/backlink work that establishes the Metairie footprint as its own market entity rather than a Baton Rouge spillover.

⚠
Defending and extending the lead

The 20× traffic lead over the nearest Baton Rouge competitor is impressive but not durable without sustained content velocity, technical hygiene, and local-link growth. Needs help with the ongoing monthly content cadence, schema maintenance as new pages launch, and Baton Rouge / LSU / community-partnership backlink work that holds and widens the gap.
