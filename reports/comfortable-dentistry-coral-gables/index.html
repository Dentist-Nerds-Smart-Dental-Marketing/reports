Executive Summary · Your Questions, Answered

Key Findings

#1 Weakness — the entire practice rests on one page
91% of all organic traffic comes from /services/veneers/, which ranks #6 for "veneers coral gables." The IV sedation page brings the other 9%. Every other page on the site — implants, clear aligners, root canal, general dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, BOTOX, bone grafting, the homepage itself — earns zero organic visits. That's not a traffic problem; it's a single point of failure.
What's working — a 5.0★ rating across 397 reviews
This is the strongest asset in the audit and it is not close. A perfect 5.0 with 397 reviews puts Comfortable Dentistry ahead of essentially every Coral Gables competitor on trust — and reviews are one of the primary signals both Google's Map Pack and AI assistants weigh when recommending a dentist. Nothing here needs fixing. It needs to be made visible.
The money pages rank — just below page 1, where nobody clicks
Sedation is the practice's differentiator and it sits at #11, #12 and #14 for "sedation dentist / sedation dentistry / sleep dentistry coral gables" — one to four positions from page 1. Implants sit at #21 and #28, Invisalign at #28, BOTOX at #18, "teeth in a day" at #29. These aren't rankings that need to be built from nothing; they're rankings that need a push. Positions 11–15 moving to 5–8 is the single fastest traffic gain available.
The site ranks for other dentists' names and towns 300 miles away
Semrush shows the domain ranking for "palencia dental," "randy furshman dds," "dr dempsey dentist," "hennessey dentist," "encore dental of lacey reviews," plus "iv sedation the villages fl," "sinus lift surgery oviedo fl" and "iv sedation zuber fl." None of these are Coral Gables and none are this practice. That pattern is the signature of templated, vendor-supplied page copy shared across many dental websites — Google recognizes the text as generic, which is a direct brake on ranking for the terms that do matter.
131 referring domains, Authority Score 6
215 backlinks from 131 domains should buy more than a 6/100 Authority Score. That mismatch is the classic signature of directory-grade, low-value links that don't pass real strength. Until the domain earns genuine authority, the page-2 rankings above will keep sticking — authority is what pushes position 11 to position 5.
Coral Gables searches in Spanish. The site only speaks English.
The site already ranks #21 for "rellenos blancos coral gables" and #62 for "dentista sedación coral gables" (210/mo) — purely by accident, since there is not a single Spanish page in the sitemap and the only declared language is hreflang="en". In a market where a large share of patients search in Spanish, that's demand arriving at a door with no room behind it.
Six core services have real local demand and no page at all
Teeth whitening (390/mo), emergency dentist (170/mo), periodontist (170/mo), dental crowns (140/mo), dentures (110/mo) and wisdom teeth removal (30/mo) add up to 1,010 Coral Gables searches every month that this site cannot compete for, because no page exists to compete with. Each of these 404s today.

Triage Center

Usability & Technical Issues

0/17
FAQ Schema Missing — the AI visibility gap, verified
We fetched the raw HTML of six key pages (veneers, implants, IV sedation, clear aligners, BOTOX, about) and grepped for FAQPage markup. Zero hits on every one. The practice has published 17 real patient-question pages — "How does IV sedation differ from oral sedation and nitrous oxide?", "How long do I wait after a bone graft before the final implant crown?" — which is exactly the content AI assistants reward. Without FAQPage schema, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews can't parse it as answers. This is a markup job on content that already exists, not a writing job.
397
No Review Schema — 397 five-star reviews are invisible to search
The site carries Dentist, Service, PostalAddress, GeoCoordinates, OpeningHours and BreadcrumbList schema — the local basics are in place. What's missing is AggregateRating / Review markup. The single most persuasive fact about this practice — 5.0★ from 397 patients — can't appear as stars in search results and isn't available to AI engines summarizing local dentists.
3×
Inconsistent Business Name — three versions in play
Google Business Profile says "Comfortable Dentistry of Coral Gables." The website brands itself "Comfortable Dentistry." The domain is truecomfortdentist.com. Mixed naming splits brand-search signal and weakens entity recognition for both Google and AI engines. Pick one official name and use it identically across the site, schema, GBP and every directory listing.
8
Templated Vendor Copy — ranking for other practices and other states
Eight of the domain's ranking keywords belong to other businesses or far-away markets: "palencia dental," "randy furshman dds," "dr dempsey dentist," "hennessey dentist," "encore dental of lacey reviews," "iv sedation the villages fl," "sinus lift surgery oviedo fl," "iv sedation zuber fl." Boilerplate copy shared across a website vendor's client base reads as generic to Google — which caps how well any of it can rank for Coral Gables.
ES
No Spanish Content in a Bilingual Market
The sitemap contains 56 URLs and not one is Spanish; the site declares hreflang="en" only. Yet it already ranks #21 for "rellenos blancos coral gables" and #62 for "dentista sedación coral gables" (210/mo) on English pages alone. Spanish treatment pages would open a lane most Coral Gables competitors have also left empty.
6
Missing Service Pages — 1,010 searches a month with nowhere to land
We tested them directly: /services/teeth-whitening, /services/emergency-dentistry, /services/periodontal-treatment, /services/crowns-bridges, /services/dentures and /services/all-on-4 all return 404. Together those services carry 1,010 Coral Gables searches a month. Six new pages is a fortnight of work and roughly doubles the site's addressable market.
