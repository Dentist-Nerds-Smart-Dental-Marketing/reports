ISSUE 1 Critical · Ownership Risk

The website is rented, not owned — any SEO equity built today could disappear tomorrow.

The platform that built lpsmiles.com operates on a model where the website asset belongs to them. Cancel, and the site goes dark. This creates a fundamental problem: every dollar spent on SEO, every backlink earned, every page that climbs the rankings — it's all built on infrastructure the practice doesn't own. Most dental practices don't realize this until it's too late.

We know this company well. Their business model depends on retention through dependency: the more SEO work they do on your site, the harder it becomes to leave — because leaving means starting over at zero. That's by design.

✓ The Fix

Build a new website on a platform you own outright — on your own hosting, with your own domain, with full code access. When you invest in SEO after that, every keyword gained and every backlink earned is a permanent asset of the practice, not the vendor. This is the single most important structural change.

ISSUE 2 Critical · Keyword Volume

49 keywords and 8 monthly visitors after nearly a year online — far below where this practice should be.

After roughly 11 months live, lpsmiles.com ranks for only 49 keywords and attracts about 8 organic visits per month. A healthy dental practice website in a market like La Puente should be at 300–1,000+ keywords within the first year. The site's top keyword is "dentist in la puente ca" at position 4 — that's genuinely good for a new domain — but it's almost the only thing driving any traffic at all. Service pages rank for nothing independently because they lack the content depth and FAQ structure needed to target treatment-specific searches.

For comparison, pareshkumar.com (a direct local competitor) ranks for 241 keywords and drives 189 monthly visitors. lapuenteemergency.com ranks for 172 keywords and drives 89 monthly visitors. The gap is significant but entirely closeable.

✓ The Fix

Every service page needs to be rebuilt with genuine depth: 600–1,200 words of original content per page, local geographic signals (La Puente, West Covina, Baldwin Park, Covina, Rowland Heights), a full FAQ section with schema markup on each treatment page, and internal linking between related services. Each service page should be able to rank independently for its own treatment-specific local keywords — that's how you go from 49 to 400+ keywords.

ISSUE 3 High · Domain Authority

Domain Authority of 6 limits how high any page can rank, no matter how good the content is.

Domain Authority (DA) is a measure of how much Google trusts a website, based largely on how many credible external sites link to it. At DA 6, lpsmiles.com has very little link equity — meaning Google treats it as a new, unproven domain. In competitive local searches where competitors have built authority over years, a DA 6 site is fighting with one hand tied behind its back.

This is separate from content quality — a page can be perfectly written but still not rank if the domain doesn't have enough authority to compete. Building backlinks from local directories, dental associations, community organizations, and relevant local businesses is essential to improve this score.

✓ The Fix

Launch a structured local authority campaign: submit to all major dental and local business directories (Yelp, Healthgrades, ZocDoc, Google Business Profile, Nextdoor Business, Chamber of Commerce, Bing Places), pursue mentions in local La Puente community media and neighborhood sites, and earn links from dental association member directories. Target DA 20+ within 12 months — a realistic goal with consistent effort.

ISSUE 4 High · Content Authenticity

Copy-paste blog content actively hurts rankings — Google's Helpful Content system targets exactly this.

The blog content currently on the site appears to be templated, generic, and not specific to this practice or this community. Google's Helpful Content algorithm specifically evaluates whether content was written primarily to help people or primarily to rank in search engines. Templated, copy-pasted dental blog posts that could appear on any practice's website are a red flag — they can suppress the entire domain's rankings, not just the blog pages.

The irony is that the practice almost certainly knows things a generic template never would: what patients in La Puente specifically ask about, what insurances are accepted locally, what makes this neighborhood different from nearby cities. That kind of authentic, localized content is exactly what Google rewards.

✓ The Fix

Replace all templated blog content with original, practice-specific articles written for the La Puente community. Minimum 4 blogs per month, covering local search intent: "best dentist near La Puente CA," "dental implants cost West Covina," "emergency dentist La Puente open Saturday," "Medi-Cal dentist La Puente." Each blog should include the doctors' names, specific services, and local neighborhood references — content that only this practice could have written.

ISSUE 5 High · Conversion & Trust

Stock imagery throughout the site reduces trust and conversion — patients want to see the real practice before they book.

When a prospective patient visits a dental website, the photos tell them whether they can trust the practice. Generic stock photos of smiling models and dental tools look identical to the stock photos on thousands of other dental websites — they signal nothing unique about this practice, this team, or this office.

Real photography of the La Puente Smiles team, the treatment rooms, the reception area, and the actual equipment builds immediate credibility. Patients in La Puente who are searching for a neighborhood dentist want to see a neighborhood dentist — not a photoshoot model who's never been in this zip code.

✓ The Fix

Invest in a professional photography session at the office. Photograph the team, the operatories, the reception area, and any technology or equipment the practice uses. Replace every stock image on the site — starting with the homepage hero banner — with real photos. On the new website we build for you, real photography will be a core design requirement, not an afterthought.
