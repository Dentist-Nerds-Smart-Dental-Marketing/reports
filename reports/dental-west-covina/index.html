Executive Summary · Your Questions, Answered

Key Findings

#1 Weakness — 91% of traffic is one branded search
The single biggest issue: "west covina perfect smile dentistry" drives 91% of all visits. The site ranks for real money terms — but they're stuck on page 2–3, including the market's top term "dentist in west covina ca" (1,000/mo, $15.72 CPC) at position #17. Almost no new patient who doesn't already know the name can find the practice. Pushing those pages onto page 1 is the highest-leverage move available.
Traffic is 70% below its 2025 peak — and volatile
Organic visits peaked at 192/mo in March 2025, then slid to the low 40s by year-end. A brief spike in Feb–Apr 2026 (up to 141) has already faded back to ~57. The swings point to rankings clustered on page 2 that drift in and out of view rather than holding firm.
98% of traffic enters the homepage
The homepage carries 98% of organic visits. The only other page with any traffic is an off-topic blog about a national aligner brand. The dozen treatment pages — implants, Invisalign, veneers, oral surgery — each bring essentially zero.
Thousands of backlinks — but low quality
The site has 9,300+ backlinks from 339 referring domains, yet Authority Score is stuck at 8/100. That mismatch is the signature of low-quality, repetitive links that don't pass real authority. Quality links (and likely pruning toxic ones) are what will move rankings.
What's working — #1 brand ranking and a 5.0★ reputation
The practice ranks #1 for its brand and holds an outstanding 5.0★ rating across ~144 reviews (detailed in the GBP scorecard below) — stronger than most West Covina competitors. The reputation and the service-page library are real assets to build on, not start from scratch.

Triage Center

Usability & Technical Issues

UX
Navigation Menu — the site's most critical usability gap
The navigation is the biggest usability problem on the site: patients can't easily find the treatment pages from the menu, so even the pages that exist go unseen — and a page nobody can reach is a page Google has little reason to rank. A clear, conventional nav (Services with each treatment listed, About, Reviews, Contact, and a prominent "Book" button) is the single highest-impact usability fix.
4
Inconsistent Business Name — unify it across the entire site
The practice refers to itself by several names — "West Covina Perfect Smile Dentistry," "Perfect Smile Dental Services," and "Dental West Covina" all appear. Mixed naming confuses patients, splits brand search signals, and weakens entity recognition for Google and AI. Pick one official name and use it identically everywhere: logo, page titles, footer, schema, and every directory listing.
3
URL Cannibalization — pages competing against each other
Single terms are chased by several URLs at once — "teeth in a day covina" ranks with /snap-on-denture, /dental-implant and /oral-surgery; "smile center west covina" with the homepage, /about_us and /oral-surgery. Google splits the signal and none reach page 1. Consolidating to one canonical page per term is a fast ranking lift.
4,400
Off-Topic Content — ranking for a national aligner brand, not patients
The only non-homepage page with any traffic is a blog comparing "AlignerCo," a national direct-to-consumer aligner brand (4,400/mo). It pulls people researching a mail-order product — not West Covina patients looking for a dentist. That content effort would convert far better aimed at local treatment searches.
