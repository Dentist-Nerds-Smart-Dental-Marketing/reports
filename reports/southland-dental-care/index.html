Biggest Priorities

Top 3 Weaknesses

The three issues doing the most damage right now. Two of them can be fixed within days.

01

The specialist site is switched off

periodonticsdentalimplants.com tells Google not to index any of its 15 pages — including the page Dr. Hanookai's Google profile links to. This is almost certainly WordPress's “Discourage search engines” box, left ticked after launch: a one-click fix.

Critical
02

A spam attack on all three domains

About 4,800 spam domains pointed at the three sites in one September week, with anchors like “buy backlinks online cheap”. Between 95% and 99% of each site's referring domains are now near-worthless. All three need a disavow.

Critical
03

The practice competes with itself

Two Google profiles at the same address share the identical primary category, four phone numbers appear across profiles and sites, and the brand search lands on six different Southland pages. Result: a 4% share of the map for “dentist sherman oaks”.

Critical
Other issues we found
  • “dentist sherman oaks” (590 searches/mo) ranks #67 — on the location page, not the homepage
  • 76 tag, category and paginated archive pages are open to Google and cannibalize core pages
  • Blog content targets Los Angeles (48 URLs) over Sherman Oaks (2), so traffic isn't local patients
  • The 2017 site says “over 15 years of experience”; the new site says “28+ years”
  • No site meets Google's 2.5 s mobile load target (5.1 s · 4.0 s · 7.2 s)
  • Southland never links to the specialist site, and the specialist site has no canonical tags
  • A third listing, “Dental Implant Center”, sits at the same address with no reviews or website
  • 295 page titles run past 60 characters; 78 pages share duplicate meta descriptions

Our Game Plan

How Dentist Nerds Fixes This

We don't believe in flashy launches that flame out. The plan starts with a strong foundation, then layers ongoing engines on top — each one compounding the last.

⚡ Start Here
01
The Foundation
Consolidate the practice's web presence.

What we'd do in Step 1

No rebuild is needed — the sites are well built. Step 1 is about making three websites and two Google profiles work as one practice instead of against each other. Most of it is configuration, not construction, and the first two items can be done within days.

  • Switch the specialist site back on. Untick “Discourage search engines” on periodonticsdentalimplants.com, add canonical tags, and submit its sitemap in Search Console. This is a same-day fix.
  • Disavow the spam on all three domains. One disavow file per site covering the ~4,800 spam domains — while keeping the specialist site's genuine 2023–2024 links.
  • Retire delaramhanookai.com. It ranks for nothing, runs 2017-era WordPress and contradicts the new site's credentials. Once its spam is disavowed, redirect its pages to the matching specialist-site pages.
  • Give each site one job. Southland for general and cosmetic dentistry; the specialist site for periodontics, implants and referring dentists. Link them to each other both ways.
  • Untangle the two Google profiles. Southland's primary category becomes “Dentist”; Dr. Hanookai's becomes “Periodontist”. One main phone per profile, matching its website. Check whether the third “Dental Implant Center” listing belongs to the practice.
  • Stop the cannibalization. Close the 76 tag, category and paginated archives to Google, fix the pagination canonicals, and point brand searches at the homepage.
  • Get under 2.5 seconds. Move the inline CSS into a cached stylesheet and serve next-gen images on all three sites.

Why this matters: today the practice's best site is invisible, its authority is buried under spam, and its two profiles split the same searches. Fixing that first means every review, link and article afterwards adds up instead of cancelling out.
