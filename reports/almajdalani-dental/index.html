Executive Summary

Key Findings

Only 45 Ranking Keywords — Target Is 400+
Semrush live data confirms almajdalanidental.com ranks for just 45 keywords — nearly all branded searches for variations of Dr. Almajdalani's name. A Bay Area dental practice offering implants, full-mouth reconstruction, oral surgery, orthodontics, and cosmetic dentistry should command 400+ keywords. Every missing keyword is a patient who found a competitor instead.
Too Many Stock Images — Hurting Conversion Rate
The website relies heavily on generic dental stock photography — identical smiling models, sterile generic operatory images, and artificial-looking stock teeth. Patients comparing practices immediately recognize the difference between stock and real. Authentic photography of the actual team, office, and results increases conversion rates by 30–40% over stock imagery.
Website Is Not ADA Compliant
The site does not meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA accessibility standards. In California, this creates legal exposure under the Unruh Civil Rights Act. Beyond legal risk, Google uses accessibility as a user-experience ranking signal — non-compliant sites receive lower rankings across all categories, hurting organic performance.
Treatment Pages Missing H 1 Tags — No Heading Structure
Service and treatment pages on almajdalanidental.com lack proper H1, H2, and H3 heading tags. Search engines rely on heading hierarchy to understand what a page covers and how to rank it. Without that structure, Google cannot correctly categorize treatment pages — they remain invisible for the procedures Dr. Almajdalani actually performs.
No FAQ Content — Zero AI Search Visibility
ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews surface practices by reading FAQ-style, question-and-answer content. With no FAQ sections on any treatment page, Dr. Almajdalani's practice does not appear when AI assistants recommend Bay Area dental specialists — an increasingly primary discovery channel for new patients.
Only 25 Pages — Dozens of Treatment Pages Missing
With only 25 indexed pages, the site is missing dedicated pages for many high-value procedures that Dr. Almajdalani offers. Each missing treatment page is a missing ranking opportunity. A complete dental practice site needs 100+ properly optimized pages to compete for all the relevant local search queries in the Bay Area market.

Website Audit

Critical Issues Identified

Usability Issues
Website Has Usability Issues Blocking New Patients
Navigation friction, unclear calls to action, and page layout issues make it harder for visitors to find information or book an appointment. Every extra click or moment of confusion increases bounce rate and reduces the chance a potential new patient contacts the office.
Stock Photos
Too Many Stock Images — Hurting Conversion
Heavy reliance on generic stock dental photography erodes patient trust. Patients evaluating practices want to see the real doctor, real team, real office, and real patient results. Stock imagery signals "generic" — authentic photography drives significantly higher appointment booking rates.
Not ADA Compliant
Website Does Not Meet ADA Accessibility Standards
The site fails WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance standards. This creates legal liability under California law and signals to Google that the user experience is below standard. ADA compliance is a ranking factor and a legal requirement — non-compliant dental sites in California face ongoing risk.
No H Tags
Treatment Pages Missing H1 / H2 / H3 Heading Structure
Service and treatment pages lack proper heading tags. Without structured headings, Google cannot correctly categorize what each treatment page covers or match it to patient search queries. This suppresses rankings even for services the practice actively offers and is one of the most impactful on-page fixes available.
No FAQ
No FAQ Content — Invisible to AI Search Engines
FAQ sections with structured FAQ schema are how AI engines discover and cite dental practices. ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews read FAQ content to recommend specialists. Zero FAQ content across the entire site means zero AI-driven new patient referrals — a channel that is growing rapidly in dental.
45 of 400+ Keywords
Only 45 Keywords Ranking — 355+ Opportunities Unreachable
Semrush data shows 45 total ranking keywords with almost all traffic coming from branded name searches. A fully optimized Bay Area dental practice should rank for 400+ keywords across treatment types, locations, conditions, and procedure questions. The gap between 45 and 400 is the gap between a site people find only by name and one that actively grows the practice.
