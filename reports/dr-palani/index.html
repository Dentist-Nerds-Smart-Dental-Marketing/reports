Other Key Findings · What Needs Improvement

⚠
Only two local commercial signals producing traffic

Dentist palos verdes sits at #6 (110/mo) and no credit check dental implant financing at #7 (320/mo). These are the only non-brand commercial queries currently producing meaningful visits. Every other implant, veneer, sedation, and cosmetic query the practice should be winning is dormant.

⚠
National blog audience doesn't convert to RPV patients

Top traffic pages (coconut oil for toothache, super glue on teeth, best gum for breath, fluoride allergy, bruising after wisdom teeth) attract national informational searchers — not Rancho Palos Verdes implant prospects. 70%+ of traffic is the wrong audience for the practice's revenue model.

⚠
Service pages missing key conversion elements

Implant, veneer, sedation, and cosmetic pages lack embedded patient testimonials, before/after galleries, transparent cost ranges, and procedure-specific FAQ sections. Even if traffic arrives, pages aren't built to convert it into consultation requests.

↓
Service-page internal-link density is weak

The high-traffic blog posts don't link to matching service pages (coconut oil post → no link to /implant or /general dentistry pages, super glue post → no link to /emergency). Authority arrives via the blog but can't reach commercial pages.

⚠
Reputation signal not surfacing in search results

120 Google reviews at 4.8★ plus Dr. Palani's FICOI implant-specialty credential are real assets — but with no schema markup on any page, none of this appears in Google's rich results, AI Overviews, or knowledge panels. Reputation is invisible to the channels that increasingly drive clicks.
