WEBSITE ANALYSIS

What's Holding the Site Back

We reviewed seattlebiomimeticdentistry.com in detail. You built it on Squarespace, and you did a great job — the design shows real care for the brand. But based on our experience with hundreds of dental websites, several factors are making it very hard for this site to attract new patients or convert the ones who do find it. Here's what we found:

🎮
Typography That Fights the Brand
The font choices read more like a gaming or tech startup than a trusted healthcare provider. Patients searching for a dentist need to feel safe, professional, and cared for the moment they land. Typography is one of the first signals that builds — or breaks — that trust in under two seconds.
📐
Layout & Calls-to-Action Need Rebuilding
The page layout doesn't guide visitors toward a phone call or booking. Clear CTAs — "Book an Appointment," "Call Us Today," "Request a Consultation" — need to appear in the hero, mid-page, and footer. Right now, patients who land on the site have no obvious next step.
📄
No Treatment / Service Pages
Google ranks individual service pages — not homepages — for specific searches like "biomimetic dentist Seattle," "tooth-colored fillings Seattle," or "CEREC same-day crowns Seattle." Without dedicated pages for each treatment, you can't rank for any of those searches, no matter how good the homepage is.
⌨️
Not Written for Search Engines
SEO is largely a text-based game. Google reads words. Right now, the site doesn't have enough written content explaining your services, your philosophy, or your credentials in a way that Google can index. A great website needs rich, keyword-targeted copy on every page so search engines understand exactly what you offer and who you serve.
Note on Squarespace: Squarespace is a capable platform, but it has real limitations for SEO — from how it handles page structure and schema markup to restrictions on custom code and page speed optimization. For a practice that wants to compete seriously on Google, a purpose-built website gives you far more control and better results.
