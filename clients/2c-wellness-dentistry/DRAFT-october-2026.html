Semrush Position Tracking

Keyword Rankings

A note on this month's reference date

From October 27 onward Semrush stopped returning positions for most of this campaign: the number of tracked keywords with any recorded position fell from 10 of 14 to 1, then only partially recovered. Google Search Console shows those keywords were still ranking the whole time. On October 28, for example, Semrush reported no top-100 position for "dental implants near me", "emergency dentist laguna niguel" or "root canal laguna niguel", while Google recorded them at positions #13, #17.5 and #31 respectively — and "dental implants near me" went on improving to #12 and #11 over the following two days. "Veneers in laguna niguel" makes the point most plainly: #8 on October 26, absent on October 27, back at #8 on October 28. So this is a measurement gap in Semrush, not a loss of rankings. The table below therefore compares September 30 to October 26 — the last day Semrush collected the full campaign — rather than to October 31.

Ranking keywords from the practice's Semrush position-tracking campaign. Ten of the fourteen tracked terms held a top-100 position, up from eight in September. The emergency phrases were the month's clear winners — "emergency dentist laguna niguel" climbed 20 places to #18 and "emergency dentist near me" reached #14. Against that, "root canal laguna niguel" gave up its #3 spot and slid to #60, and the implant cluster drifted back. Terms marked Building sit outside the top 20.
