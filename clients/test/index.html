Executive Summary

Key Findings

📉

29 Organic Visits/Month — Near Zero Visibility

Semrush estimates only ~29 organic sessions per month. Traffic has declined over 70% from a peak of ~115/mo in early 2022. The site is functionally invisible in organic search.

🔍

Only 23 Pages — Missing Critical Treatment Pages

The entire website has just 23 pages. No dedicated pages for dental implants, crowns, bridges, root canals, veneers, Invisalign, emergency dentistry, or sedation dentistry. Competitors have 60–150+ pages.

📊

Main Keywords Ranking on Page 2–10

"Dentists in Monterey California" at position 86. "Dentist in Monterey Park CA" at position 46. "Dentist Monterey" at position 12. Zero keywords in top 3 for commercial dental terms.

🤖

Low AI / LLM Visibility

With only 23 thin pages and no schema markup beyond basic defaults, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews have very little content to cite. AI traffic is effectively zero.

⚠️

Authority Score: 7/100

Semrush Authority Score is 7 — extremely low. Only 138 referring domains, mostly low-quality directories. No authoritative backlinks from dental associations, local press, or health sites.

🏥

No Schema Markup — Missing E-E-A-T Signals

No LocalBusiness, Dentist, or Person schema on any page. Google sees a generic website — not a dental practice with a credentialed provider. Critical gap for healthcare (YMYL) sites.

🗂️ Website Architecture Issues

The site has no proper service page hierarchy. "Services" is a single page listing everything — not individual, keyword-targeted pages. Blog posts from 2016 have no internal links back to service pages. The "Welcome" page duplicates the homepage content.

📄 Content Freshness

Blog posts date from 2016–2017 and have not been updated. Google's freshness signals penalize stale content in competitive markets. Blog titles use generic topics with no local keyword targeting ("How Often to Have Teeth Cleaned" — no mention of Monterey).

📱 Mobile & Speed

Full mobile/speed audit recommended. The site appears to be a WordPress installation from 2015–2016 that may have accumulated technical debt including outdated plugins, unoptimized images, and legacy code affecting Core Web Vitals.

🔗 Internal Linking

No visible internal linking strategy. Service pages don't link to each other. Blog posts don't link to service pages. The homepage doesn't funnel link equity to the most important treatment pages. This flattens the site's topical authority.
