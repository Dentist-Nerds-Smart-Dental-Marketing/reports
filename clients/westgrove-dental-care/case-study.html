The Challenge

A trusted practice that too few people could find.

Drs. Iris and Brandon Skenandore had built exactly the kind of practice patients hope for — a modern, welcoming office with a flawless 5.0★ reputation. But reputation only works when people can find you.

Outside of branded searches, Westgrove was hard to discover. The website ranked for a modest set of keywords, the Google Business Profile was under-leveraged, and there was no paid channel actively putting the practice in front of nearby patients. In a competitive Garden Grove market, not showing up means losing patients to the practice down the street — every single day.

The goal was twofold: turn on a reliable flow of new-patient demand now through Meta Ads, while compounding long-term visibility through SEO so the practice keeps growing even when the ads pause.

The Approach

Two channels, one growth engine.

1

Locally-targeted Meta Ads

Built a Garden Grove–focused campaign on Facebook & Instagram, putting Westgrove's services in front of high-intent local audiences with creative tuned to the practice's strengths.

2

Conversion-first scaling

Started lean to learn what worked, then shifted to a conversion-optimized "Sales" objective and scaled spend into the best-performing audiences — driving cost-per-click down as volume went up.

3

Service-page & keyword SEO

Optimized core service pages around how Garden Grove patients actually search, expanded topical authority with consistent blog content, and tracked rankings across Semrush, GA4, and Local Falcon.

4

Google Business Profile & authority

Sharpened the Google Business Profile, kept reviews answered to protect the 5.0★ rating, and built links steadily to grow domain authority and local map presence.

The Results · Meta Ads

A paid channel that reaches the whole neighborhood.

From its April launch through the end of May, the Meta Ads program reached over 42,000 people across Garden Grove and scaled efficiently as it learned — May more than doubled April's reach while keeping clicks affordable.
