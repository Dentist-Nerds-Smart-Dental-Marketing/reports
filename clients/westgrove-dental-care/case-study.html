Case Study

Westgrove Dental Care

Two channels, one growth engine — Meta Ads to drive new patients now, SEO to keep them coming. Here's exactly how.

Watch Interview

Patient interview — coming soon

The Challenge

Westgrove Dental Care had a beautiful practice and a flawless 5.0★ reputation across hundreds of reviews — but outside of branded searches, too few local patients could find them. The website ranked for only a modest set of keywords, the Google Business Profile was under-leveraged, and no paid channel was actively putting the practice in front of nearby patients.

In a competitive Garden Grove market, not showing up at the top means losing patients to the practice down the street — every single day.

The Process

We focused on one goal: making Westgrove Dental Care the first practice locals find — and the easiest one to choose. The plan ran on two tracks at once, paid and organic.

1. Locally-Targeted Meta Ads

We built a Garden Grove–focused campaign across Facebook & Instagram, putting Westgrove's services in front of high-intent local audiences:

  • Tight geographic targeting around the Garden Grove service area
  • Creative built around the practice's real strengths and offers
  • Continuous testing of audiences, placements, and messaging

This gave the practice an immediate, controllable source of new-patient demand.

2. Conversion-First Scaling

We started lean to learn what worked, then scaled budget into the winners:

  • Shifted from early lead-gen testing to a conversion-optimized objective
  • Reallocated spend toward the best-performing audiences and creative
  • Kept clicks cost-efficient as daily delivery climbed

The result: a steady, growing flow of new-patient leads — up +67% month over month.

3. Service-Page & Keyword SEO

Most dental sites use clinical language — patients don't search that way. We aligned the site with how Garden Grove patients actually search:

  • Optimized core service pages around real local search terms
  • Published consistent blog content to broaden topical authority
  • Tracked positions monthly across Semrush, GA4, and Local Falcon

Organic keyword positions grew across every tier in the period:

Keywords in Google's top 20 grew from 24 to 40, and total ranking keywords more than doubled — from 65 to 146.

4. Google Business Profile & Authority

The Google Business Profile is the single most important asset for a local dental practice. We kept it sharp and growing:

  • Maintained accurate info, services, photos, and regular posts
  • Answered new reviews consistently to protect the 5.0★ rating
  • Built links steadily to grow domain authority and map presence

Today, Westgrove Dental Care shows up first on Google Maps for about half of the searches across Garden Grove.
