The Challenge

A great practice that Google was hiding

Westgrove Dental Care is a beloved, 5-star practice in Garden Grove — but online, hardly anyone could find them. Their website barely showed up when people searched for a dentist nearby, so all those potential patients were quietly being handed to competitors instead.

  • 🔍
    Almost invisible in search. The site ranked for only a small handful of terms — and not the ones people actually type when they're ready to book.
  • 🔗
    Very few websites linked to them. Google trusts businesses that other sites vouch for. Westgrove simply didn't have that web of support yet.
  • 📉
    Low website traffic. A wonderful team and perfect reviews, but not enough new people were ever landing on the website to discover them.
Where they started
65
keywords on Google (late 2025)
The goal

Get Westgrove in front of every person in Garden Grove searching for a dentist — and turn that visibility into phone calls and booked appointments.

The Process

What we actually did

No gimmicks — just the proven fundamentals that make Google (and real patients) take notice, done consistently every single month.

1

Built pages around what patients search

We rewrote and organized the website around the exact phrases real people use — like "dentist in Garden Grove" and "emergency dentist near me" — so Google clearly understands what Westgrove offers and who to show it to.

2

Supercharged the Google Business Profile

We optimized the Google Maps listing with fresh photos, posts, accurate info, and a steady review strategy. Today Westgrove ranks #1 on the map across nearly half of Garden Grove and pulled in 44 calls straight from Google in a single month.

3

Earned trust with quality backlinks

We steadily built links from other reputable websites back to Westgrove. Think of each one as a recommendation that tells Google "this practice is legit." That trust now adds up to 701 backlinks from 396 different websites.

4

Published helpful, patient-friendly content

Fresh blog articles answering the real questions patients ask — about chipped teeth, clear aligners, tooth pain and more — which keeps bringing new visitors in month after month and widens the net of keywords Westgrove ranks for.

5

Got ready for AI search

We structured everything so Westgrove shows up not just in classic Google results, but also in the new AI answers (like Google's AI Overviews) that more and more patients now rely on to pick a dentist.
